# 5.5.4 内存封禁的同类参照

<!-- ROW_MINE: lkml.messages.1831079 -->`
- 【Linux社区】2006年9月William Pitcock提交net/ipv4 allow_lowport_bind_nonsuperuser sysctl补丁以放松CAP_NET_BIND_SERVICE限制，援引FreeBSD既有实现 On other TCP stacks, such as the one included with FreeBSD, you can do the exact same thing by doing: # sysctl net.inet.ip.portrange.reservedhigh=0，并坦承 it's not as fancy as FreeBSD's，因 PROT_SOCK in af_inet.c 是常量而非可调项；BSD端口范围控制语义在Linux 2.6 CAP能力框架落地时被主动引用为API参照。

- 【Linux社区】2001年1月Linux内存管理维护者Rik van Riel（riel@conectiva.com.br）就Eric Biederman everyone except linux is trying hard to clone BSD instead of thinking through the issues ourselves 的MM批评正面回应，指名建议Linux hackers检查FreeBSD与NetBSD的VM代码：FreeBSD保留Mach过度工程化的抽象层但拥有绝对强悍的页面置换策略，NetBSD则清理了抽象层为低开销实现但页面置换较简单；结论 It might just be the case that we DON'T want to reinvent the wheel (that others have made into a nice round shape with 15 years of trial, error and redesigning)；Rik个人签名 Virtual memory is like a game you can't win; However, without VM there's truly nothing to lose；Linux内核MM维护者公开请求以BSD VM为参照的一手证据。
