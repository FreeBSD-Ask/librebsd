## 二、取证要点

1. **3.1.1 位移**：`Netflix adopted FreeBSD-CURRENT for its Open Connect CDN…`（系统作为 CDN 内部条件）＋`Open Connect is Netflix’s CDN…`（对外单位是服务与设备）。
2. **3.1.2 选型惯例**：`Netflix has an internal “distro”`（自建发行版）＋`Each server is designed to hold between 10TB and 120TB…10,000 to 40,000 simultaneous long-lived TCP sessions`（并发与容量需求）。
3. **3.1.5 规模压力**：`Video quality is of the utmost importance, so we don’t transcode on the server`（负载简化）＋`we get about 375 gigs at about 53% busy CPU with FreeBSD`（内存带宽瓶颈与运行状态）。
