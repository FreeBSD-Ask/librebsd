## 二、取证要点

1. **3.7.1 企业与项目的技术流动**：基金会案例研究叙述 Netflix `carefully manages the code flow between the in-house FreeBSD implementation and the wider FreeBSD community`（企业与项目间的流动被组织为受控代码流）；NetApp 侧 `contributes to FreeBSD in many ways, including periodically hosting FreeBSD Foundation events, making regular bug fixes and enhancements, and providing financial support to the FreeBSD Foundation`（回馈呈活动／缺陷修复增强／资金赞助多渠道形态）。两源互证：流动主体是代码与补丁，另有非代码渠道并列。
2. **3.7.2 提交者身份的双重归属**：`Gleb Smirnoff is a skilled software engineer and experienced FreeBSD committer who works at Netflix and manages the customized and performance-optimized FreeBSD-based firmware for Open Connect`（同一人兼具提交者与公司工程师双重身份）；NetApp 侧 `NetApp's engineers have also upstreamed new technology to FreeBSD in the past, like bhyve, the BSD-licensed hypervisor`（企业工程师以上游贡献者身份回送新技术）。命名当事人＋企业工程师群体两侧互证。
3. **3.7.3 商业需求进入路线图的方式**：`When the test encounters issues with FreeBSD, NetApp reports them through Klara, and Klara changes upstream`（商业缺陷与需求经商业支持承包商转译后进入上游）；当事人自述 `Tracking FreeBSD-CURRENT … allowed us to collaborate with upstream developers and get our changes into FreeBSD quickly`（直接协作渠道使改动快速到达）。两形态对照：承包商转译渠道与直接协作渠道并存，方式决定到达速度。
