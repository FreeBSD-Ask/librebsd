1. **1.8.1 设备化转向**：`The OS became nothing but a necessary cog in the stack.`（系统沦为齿轮）＋`An operating system, like the hardware that runs it, is now just thought of as part of the “infrastructure”.`（系统与硬件同列）。两证均为厂商自述的 post-OS 论，落块时明确“叙述依据来自厂商”，不写成本书的事实断言。
2. **1.8.4 从发行版到固件**：`we simply re-numbered the 13.1 release to 13.3 to align with its updated FreeBSD 13.3 operating system`（版本号单方重编）＋`Today, we are announcing our plans to release TrueNAS CORE 13.3 in the next few months.`（发版由厂商宣告）。合成“版本、补丁与升级由厂商单方掌握”。
