## 二、取证要点

1. **3.8.1 “使用 FreeBSD”与继承 BSD 技术的分野**：IBM 红皮书记厂商以 FreeBSD 为 Data ONTAP 8.0 的操作系统（`The use of FreeBSD as the operating system for Data ONTAP 8.0 allows for some significant benefits. Besides the fact that Data ONTAP will continuously benefit from the third-party work taking place within the FreeBSD community`——整套系统为底座的使用形态）；ONTAP 9.18.1P8 notice report 明示组件清单按版本分发且所列不必尽用（`This product does not necessarily use all the software components referred to below`）——组件级继承叙述有独立于部署叙述的公开载体。两源互证两种记载形态的分野。
2. **3.8.5 判定标准的适用边界**：NetApp KB 把闭源产品的组件细节统一指向法律通告节（`see Legal notices section of ONTAP Documentation`）；法律通告节自述职能（`Notice files provide information about third-party copyright and licenses used in NetApp software.`）。判定链＝KB 指针→通告节→按版本 notice 文件，闭源设备的判定边界落在披露面的可得性上。
