### 新闻条目 3

- **新闻标题（中英）**：The History of the NetBSD Project — Founding and portability focus（NetBSD 项目历史 —— 成立与可移植性定位）
- **新闻来源**：NetBSD Project 官方网站 + NetBSD 项目维护者集体署名 + 持续维护至 2026 年 + **<https://www.netbsd.org/about/history.html>** + 访问日期 2026-07-27
- **新闻摘要**：NetBSD 官方历史页面记载：“NetBSD took its roots from the original UCB 4.3BSD via the Net/2 release and 386BSD. The NetBSD project was founded by Chris Demetriou, Theo de Raadt, Adam Glass and Charles M. Hannum. Frustration with the quality of patches in the wild and the inability to get patches included in 386BSD led to the founding of the NetBSD project in 1993. NetBSD's original focus was quality and architecture independence. FreeBSD was formed later with a focus on the i386 PC platform.”
- **与本章主题的关联说明**：NetBSD 官方历史明确记载三大项目的定位分化：NetBSD 以“quality and architecture independence”（质量与架构无关性）为定位，FreeBSD 以“i386 PC platform”为定位，OpenBSD（由原 NetBSD 联合创始人 Theo de Raadt 分叉）以安全为定位。三足鼎立格局的定位分化在 1993-1996 年间形成。
- **与官方史料的交叉验证结论**：FreeBSD Handbook 第 1.3.1 节印证 FreeBSD 起源于 386BSD Patchkit 三位协调员（Nate Williams、Rod Grimes、Jordan Hubbard）的“interim cleanup snapshot”计划。OpenBSD FAQ 第 1 章历史章节印证 OpenBSD 由 Theo de Raadt 自 NetBSD 1.0 分叉。三源一致，三足鼎立格局的形成路径相互印证。

### 新闻条目 4

- **新闻标题（中英）**：FreeBSD Handbook Chapter 1.3.1 — A Brief History of FreeBSD（FreeBSD 手册第 1.3.1 节 —— FreeBSD 简史）
- **新闻来源**：FreeBSD Project 官方文档 + FreeBSD Documentation Project 集体署名 + 持续维护至 2026 年 + **<https://docs.freebsd.org/en/books/handbook/introduction/>** + 访问日期 2026-07-27
- **新闻摘要**：FreeBSD 手册第 1.3.1 节记载：“The FreeBSD Project had its genesis in the early part of 1993, partially as the brainchild of the Unofficial 386BSD Patchkit's last 3 coordinators: Nate Williams, Rod Grimes and Jordan Hubbard... they adopted the name ‘FreeBSD’ coined by David Greenman... The first CD-ROM (and general net-wide) distribution was FreeBSD 1.0, released in December of 1993. This was based on the 4.3BSD-Lite ('Net/2') tape from U.C. Berkeley... FreeBSD then set about the arduous task of literally re-inventing itself from a completely new and rather incomplete set of 4.4BSD-Lite bits... in December it released FreeBSD 2.0 to the world... followed by the more robust and easier to install FreeBSD 2.0.5 release in June of 1995.”
- **与本章主题的关联说明**：FreeBSD 手册提供 FreeBSD 自 1993 至 1995 年的完整演进路径，与 NetBSD 历史与 OpenBSD 历史形成三足鼎立格局中 FreeBSD 一角的当事人叙事。
- **与官方史料的交叉验证结论**：FreeBSD 2.0.5 ALPHA 发行说明印证 1995-06 时点与 4.4BSD-Lite 重构叙事。三足鼎立格局中 FreeBSD 的演进路径与 NetBSD、OpenBSD 的同期演进路径形成多源印证。
