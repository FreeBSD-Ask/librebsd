# 93.5 外部批评与反对者 **：(1)** VMware 开源博客（2018.2.27）——"The Story of Containers"一文（blogs.vmware.com/opensource/2018/02/27/the-story-of-containers/）梳理容器演化史时直言 FreeBSD Jails 存在"severe restrictions"——"a jail could only have one IPv4 address for communication and could only use UDP or TCP for communication (no raw sockets, no IPv6, etc.)"，并指出 FreeBSD 后期放宽限制是"In response to the emergence of not so restricting container capabilities in other operating systems"即在外部容器生态压力下的被动跟进而非主动演进
