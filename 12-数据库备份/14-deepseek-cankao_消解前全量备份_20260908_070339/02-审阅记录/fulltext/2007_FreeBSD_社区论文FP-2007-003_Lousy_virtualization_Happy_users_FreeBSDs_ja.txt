Calling chroot(2) in ftpd(1) implemented
”anonymous FTP” without the hazzle of
file/pathname parsing and editing.
”anonymous FTP” became used as a tool to
enhance network security.
By inference, chroot(2) became seen as a
security enhancing feature.
...The source were not strong in those.
