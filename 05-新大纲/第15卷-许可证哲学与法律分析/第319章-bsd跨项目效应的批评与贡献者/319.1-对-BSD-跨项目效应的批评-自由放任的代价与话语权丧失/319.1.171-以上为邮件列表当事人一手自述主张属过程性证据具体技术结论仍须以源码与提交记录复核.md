Linus Torvalds 回应 Theo de Raadt 对 mseal v10 的原子性异议，转贴 Open Group 规范原文作为 Linux 侧模型的依据：mprotect 条文为 "When mprotect() fails for reasons other than [EINVAL], the protections on some of the pages in the range [addr,addr+len) may have been changed"；mmap 条文为 "If mmap() fails for reasons other than [EBADF], [EINVAL], or [ENOTSUP], some of the mappings in the address range starting at addr and continuing for len bytes may have been unmapped"。他据此认为对方的原子性论证一直具有误导性，至少对 mprotect 而言 POSIX 对此明确不要求原子；同时给出跨谱系判断（原文逐字）："Maybe some atomicity rules have always been true for BSD, but they've never been true for Linux"，但坦承对 Open Group 文本的权威性并不确知（"while I don't know how authoritative that opengroup thing is, it's what google found"）。
