# 319.1.167 年1月mseal提案讨论中OpenBSD创始人TheodeRaadt明确反对该设

2024年1月 mseal() 提案讨论中，OpenBSD 创始人 Theo de Raadt 明确反对该设计。他在本线程早前消息中写道：“OpenBSD won't be doing this. I had PROT_IMMUTABLE as a draft. In my research I found basically zero circumstances when you userland does that. The most common circumstance is you create a RW mapping, fill it, and then change to a more restrictve mapping, and lock it.”关于内核侧锁定，他另有一句独立表述：“There are a few regions in the addressspace that can be locked while RW. For instance, the stack. But the kernel does that, not userland. I found regions where the kernel wants to do this to the address space, but there is no need to export useless functionality to userland.”针对提案方“userland 有真实需求”的说法，他表示：“I spent a year engineering a complete system which exercises the maximum amount of memory you can lock. I saw nothing like what you are describing. I had PROT_IMMUTABLE in my drafts, and saw it turning into a dangerous anti-pattern.”并直言：“Are you joking? You don't have any code doing that today. More feelings?”本封他新增的论证是：预计 glibc 将来不会有用例能以 mmap+immutable 或 mprotect+immutable 形式出现，因为 RELRO 的 mprotect 发生得相当晚、与分配的时序错开；在 OpenBSD 中唯一能在建立映射同时置 immutable 的情形是 execve 为新进程的栈区操作，而那是内核代码而非暴露给用户态的 syscall API；他还认为可以暂缓加入 PROT_MSEAL，待需求有事实支撑时再加，并对 MAP_MSEALABLE 直言“I don't get it”。
