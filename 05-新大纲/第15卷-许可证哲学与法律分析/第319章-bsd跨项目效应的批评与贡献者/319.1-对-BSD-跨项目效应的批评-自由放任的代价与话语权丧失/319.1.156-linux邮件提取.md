2000-12-21，Daniel Phillips 回复 Matthew Dillon 关于 FreeBSD pageout daemon 的报告，提出应把 clean 与 dirty 分成两套 LRU 清单，以便分别调整二者从 active 迁往 inactive 的速率；理由是猜错一个 dirty 页的活跃度代价是双倍的（写再加读）而 clean 页只需读。他原本还想建议对二者采用不同的老化速率，随即意识到不必如此：一个 inactive_dirty 页在被淘汰前有机会二次激活——先在其自身的 inactive_dirty 阶段、再在被挪到 inactive_clean 阶段，已天然获得双倍观察期。该 FreeBSD 交流构成 Linux 2.5 随后采用 clean 与 dirty LRU 分离的背景材料。

2000年12月，Daniel Phillips 在与 Matthew Dillon 就 FreeBSD 页清理守护进程的讨论中对比两套页回收机制。Phillips 观察 FreeBSD 的做法是“it still looks like he's keeping dirty pages 'on probation' twice as long as before”，即让脏页在 inactive_dirty 队列多走一轮，而不是仅放慢扫描；Dillon 对此说明机制差异：放慢扫描仍会让脏页与干净页保持相同的相对优先级，而“Giving the dirty pages an extra lap around the inactive queue gives clean pages a significantly higher priority over dirty pages in regards to choosing which page to launder next. So there is a big difference there.”Dillon 从代价角度论证该多轮方案值得：他称“'cleaning' a clean page means simply throwing it away, which costs maybe a microsecond of cpu time and no I/O.  'cleaning' a dirty page requires flushing it to its backing store prior to throwing it away, which costs a significant bit of cpu and at least one write I/O.  One write I/O may not seem like a lot, but if the disk is already loaded down and the write I/O has to seek we are talking at least 5 milliseconds of disk time eaten by the operation.”Phillips 则指出该代价差并非 Dillon 所提 1 微秒对 5 毫秒那样大，并推断“making the dirty page go 3 times around the loop should result in worse performance vs 2 times”。
