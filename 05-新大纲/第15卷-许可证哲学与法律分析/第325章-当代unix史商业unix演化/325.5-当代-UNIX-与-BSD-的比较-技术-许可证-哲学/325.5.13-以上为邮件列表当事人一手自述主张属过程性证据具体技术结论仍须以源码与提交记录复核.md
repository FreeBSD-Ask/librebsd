Carlos O'Donell 在 flock/FAGAIN/FWOULDBLOCK 讨论中称："flock is not POSIX, it's an interface invented by 4.2BSD, and was previously emulated by glibc. According to the comments in the kernel, flock(2) was added in June 1995. That's four years before parisc-linux really got going. The semantics of flock-locks and fcntl-locks are really, really different. Emulating one with the other was a really bad idea."——将 glibc 模拟方式定性为 Linux 黑暗时代的遗留。此外 Matthew 建议以"#define EWOULDBLOCK EAGAIN"在 __KERNEL__ 中强制 EAGAIN，用 EAGAIN/EWOULDBLOCK 的历史包袱解释内核为何长期两码混用。

2022年6月 statx 增加 I/O 对齐信息（STATX_IOALIGN / stx_offset_align_optimal）的讨论中，Eric Biggers 在其 2022-06-13 的消息中放弃新增该字段：“While working on the man-pages update, I'm having second thoughts about the stx_offset_align_optimal field. Does any filesystem other than XFS actually want stx_offset_align_optimal, when st[x]_blksize already exists? Many network filesystems, as well as tmpfs when hugepages are enabled, already report large (megabytes) sizes in st[x]_blksize. And all documentation I looked at (man pages for Linux, POSIX, FreeBSD, NetBSD, macOS) documents st_blksize as something like "the preferred blocksize for efficient I/O". It's never documented as being limited to PAGE_SIZE, which makes sense because it's not.”Christoph Hellwig 随即认同：“Yes. While st_blksize is utterly misnamed, it has always aways been the optimal I/O size.”因此该字段最终改为只新增 STATX_DIOALIGN。本封 Biggers 新增的内容与此无关：他提出尝试在块设备上支持 STATX_DIOALIGN，但判断该方案根本不可行，因为 inode 属于设备节点而非块设备本身（文件打开后依然如此），块设备需要另设接口——新的 BLK* ioctl 或 /sys/block/$dev/queue 下的文件。
