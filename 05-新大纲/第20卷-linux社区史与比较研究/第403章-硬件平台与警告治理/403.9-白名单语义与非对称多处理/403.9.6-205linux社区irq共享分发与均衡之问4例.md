# 403.9.6 20.5 【Linux社区】IRQ 共享、分发与均衡之问（4 例）

**要点（判定表逐字）**：Lapo TIN（2007-02-01，gmane 转发）：8×bttv IRQ 共享崩原帖——“How can I tell to the kernel to not to share the IRQ for these boards?”+ IO-APIC.txt 未解——（p23 009 Davidsen 判读的原帖，跨页对）；Erik Mouw（2007-07-02）答 Faulkner（p23 036 中断分发之问）：**“It programs the APIC. IRQs can be handled by a random CPU, by a set of CPUs, or by a single CPU. You can have some influence...by writing /proc/irq/<irqno>/smp_affinity”**——中断分发之问的结案（跨页对）；Peter H. Koenig（2002-04-16）：**“its purpose on an SMP machine is to give all processors the possibility to handle interrupts...how much difference could this make when running IO-intensive programms? single or two digit percentages?”**——IO-APIC 性能意义之问（p27 011/020 的原帖）；Robert Hancock（2007-02-02，Lapo 转贴）：**“Usually the IRQ routing is hard-wired on the motherboard, so there's no way to avoid the devices sharing IRQs. Unless the driver is badly buggy this should not cause problems anyway. What kind of video bit rate are you capturing? 8 video capture chips is a pretty heavy load on the PCI bus, maybe something just gets overwhelmed?”**——IRQ 共享硬连线论 + PCI 带宽怀疑（bttv 案三部曲之三）
