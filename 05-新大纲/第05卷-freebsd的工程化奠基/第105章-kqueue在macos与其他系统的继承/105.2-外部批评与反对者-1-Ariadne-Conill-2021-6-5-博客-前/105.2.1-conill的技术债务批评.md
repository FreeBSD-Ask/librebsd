# 105.2.1 Conill 的技术债务批评

  **要点**：Ariadne Conill 在 2021.6.5 博客 “actually, BSD kqueue is a mountain of technical debt” 中批评 kqueue 的 filter 中心化设计，每新增一种内核资源需新增一种 filter 类型，对比 epoll 的 composable 设计无需改内核，将 kqueue 定性为 “技术债务堆积如山”

  Conill 对 kqueue 技术债务的核心论断：

  > epoll can automatically monitor any kind of kernel resource without having to be modified, due to its composable design, which makes it superior to kqueue from the perspective of having less technical debt
  >
  > ——Ariadne Conill, “actually, BSD kqueue is a mountain of technical debt”, 2021-06-05
