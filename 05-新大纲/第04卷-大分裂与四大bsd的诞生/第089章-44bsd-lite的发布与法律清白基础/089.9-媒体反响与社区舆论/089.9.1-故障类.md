### 新闻条目 1

- **新闻标题（中英）**：FreeBSD Handbook Chapter 1.3.1 — FreeBSD's 4.4BSD-Lite transition（FreeBSD 手册第 1.3.1 节 —— FreeBSD 的 4.4BSD-Lite 过渡）
- **新闻来源**：FreeBSD Project 官方文档 + FreeBSD Documentation Project 集体署名（Jordan Hubbard 起草）+ 持续维护至 2026 年 + **<https://docs.freebsd.org/en/books/handbook/introduction/>** + 访问日期 2026-07-27
- **新闻摘要**：FreeBSD 手册第 1.3.1 节记载 FreeBSD 的 4.4BSD-Lite 过渡：“Around this time, some rather unexpected storm clouds formed on the horizon as Novell and U.C. Berkeley settled their long-running lawsuit over the legal status of the Berkeley Net/2 tape. A condition of that settlement was U.C. Berkeley's concession that three files of Net/2 were ‘encumbered’ code and had to be removed as they were the property of Novell, who had in turn acquired it from AT&T some time previously. What Berkeley got in return was Novell's ‘blessing’ that the 4.4BSD-Lite release, when it was finally released, would be declared unencumbered and all existing Net/2 users would be strongly encouraged to switch. This included FreeBSD, and the project was given until the end of July 1994 to stop shipping its own Net/2 based product. Under the terms of that agreement, the project was allowed one last release before the deadline, that release being FreeBSD 1.1.5.1. FreeBSD then set about the arduous task of literally re-inventing itself from a completely new and rather incomplete set of 4.4BSD-Lite bits. Although only three files having to do with System V shared memory and semaphores were removed, many other changes and bug fixes had been made to the BSD distribution, so it was a huge task to merge all the FreeBSD developments into 4.4BSD-Lite. It took the project until November of 1994 to make this transition, and in December it released FreeBSD 2.0 to the world.”
- **与本章主题的关联说明**：本章 4.4BSD-Lite 发布事件须包含 FreeBSD 阵营的过渡叙事，FreeBSD Handbook 第 1.3.1 节是 FreeBSD 项目官方对 4.4BSD-Lite 过渡的当事人叙事，构成本章 FreeBSD 阵营立场锚点。手册中“three files of Net/2 were ‘encumbered’ code”陈述与 McKusick 亲述一致；手册中“FreeBSD was given until the end of July 1994 to stop shipping its own Net/2 based product”陈述显示 FreeBSD 阵营的过渡时点。
- **与官方史料的交叉验证结论**：手册关于“1994-07 截止 Net/2 产品、1994-11 完成 4.4BSD-Lite 重构、1994-12 发布 FreeBSD 2.0”的时序与 FreeBSD 2.0.5 ALPHA 发行说明“FreeBSD 1.0 至 2.0.5 近两年”陈述一致；关于“three files of Net/2 were ‘encumbered’ code”的陈述与 McKusick 亲述“three files were removed from the 18,000 that made up Networking Release 2”一致；关于“Novell's ‘blessing’”的陈述与 McKusick 亲述“Ray Noorda, stated publicly that he would rather compete in the marketplace than in court”一致。
