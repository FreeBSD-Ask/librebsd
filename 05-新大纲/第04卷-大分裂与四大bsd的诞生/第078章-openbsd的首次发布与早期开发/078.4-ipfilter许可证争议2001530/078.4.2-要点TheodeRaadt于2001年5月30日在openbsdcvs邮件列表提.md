# 078.4.2 要点TheodeRaadt于2001年5月30日在openbsdcvs邮件列表提

  **要点**：Theo de Raadt 于 **2001 年 5 月 30 日** 在 openbsd-cvs 邮件列表提交移除 IPFilter 的日志——日志措辞激烈，包括 "baby mulching machines or atomic bombs to be dropped on Australia" 等表述；移除决定体现 OpenBSD 对自由软件准则的严格执行。移除日志原文关键表述：

> Remove ipf. Darren Reed has interpreted his (old, new, whichever) licence in a way that makes ipf not free according to the rules we established over 5 years ago...specifically, Darren says that modified versions are not permitted. But software which OpenBSD uses and redistributes must be free to all (be they people or companies), for any purpose they wish to use it, including modification, use, peeing on, or even integration into baby mulching machines or atomic bombs to be dropped on Australia

**史料**：openbsd-cvs 邮件列表 2001.5.30 提交日志原文、de Raadt 关于移除的公开声明、OpenBSD 官方 FAQ ［A］

**史观**：社区文化即技术资产：移除决定体现 OpenBSD 对自由软件准则的严格执行

**辨析**：移除日志的措辞在不同叙事中评价不一——部分视为必要强硬，部分视为过激；需对照多方公开声明核实；经[邮件列表库]验证通过：openbsd 库 openbsd-bugs 列表存 Theo de Raadt（<deraadt@cvs.openbsd.org>）2001-01-30 关闭 ipf 相关 bug 帖（Message-ID `<no-id-openbsd-bugs-c5bafa528d6676dd>`）与 openbsd-advocacy 列表 Nick Holland 2001-06-27 “pf question” 帖（Message-ID `<no-id-openbsd-advocacy-3cf29619099a77f8>`，述"IPFW was certainly on the short list... they thought PF would be better"），印证 2001 年 OpenBSD 正经历 IPFilter→PF 的防火墙迁移语境，与 2001-05-30 移除 IPFilter 记载方向一致（具体移除提交日志原文本身未在两库命中）

**文献**：openbsd-cvs 邮件列表 2001.5.30 提交日志；OpenBSD 官方 FAQ ［A］
