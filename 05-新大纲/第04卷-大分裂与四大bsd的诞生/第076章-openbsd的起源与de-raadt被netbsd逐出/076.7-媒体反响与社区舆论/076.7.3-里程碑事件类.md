# 076.7.3 里程碑事件类

### 新闻条目 1

- **新闻标题（中英）**：OpenBSD FAQ — Introduction to OpenBSD（OpenBSD 常见问答 —— OpenBSD 简介，历史章节）
- **新闻来源**：OpenBSD Project 官方网站 + OpenBSD 项目维护者集体署名 + 持续维护至 2026 年 + **<https://www.openbsd.org/faq/faq1.html>** + 访问日期 2026-07-27
- **新闻摘要**：OpenBSD FAQ 历史章节明确记载："OpenBSD was created because of a personality clash within the NetBSD project. Theo de Raadt, one of the founders of NetBSD, was forced to resign from the NetBSD core team in December of 1994. Shortly thereafter, in October of 1995, he started OpenBSD by importing the NetBSD 1.0 source tree. The first release, OpenBSD 1.2, was made available in July 1996.“该官方页面同时记载 OpenBSD 项目目标”correctness, security, standardization, and portability“，并明确”OpenBSD is thought of as the most secure UNIX-like operating system by many security professionals as a result of the never-ending comprehensive source code audit"。
- **与本章主题的关联说明**：本章核心叙事是 OpenBSD 的诞生及其“安全至上”定位的起源，OpenBSD FAQ 历史章节是 OpenBSD 项目对自身起源最权威的一手官方史料，明确指出三个关键时间点——Theo de Raadt 于 1994-12 被 NetBSD 核心团队“forced to resign”（被迫辞职）、1995-10 导入 NetBSD 1.0 源码树、1996-07 发布 OpenBSD 1.2，构成本章叙事的时间线骨架。
- **与官方史料的交叉验证结论**：OpenBSD FAQ 历史章节为 OpenBSD 项目官方史料，与 Adam Glass 1994-12-23 netbsd-users 邮件列表通知（条目 2）形成双方对照——OpenBSD 阵营措辞“forced to resign”暗示压力下的被动辞职，NetBSD 核心团队措辞“asked to resign”暗示 Core Team 主动决定。措辞差异反映双方前理解差异，史家引用时须呈现双方措辞而非统一化处理。

### 新闻条目 2

- **新闻标题（中英）**：Announcement regarding Theo de Raadt's removal from NetBSD Core Team（关于 Theo de Raadt 被请出 NetBSD 核心团队的公告）
- **新闻来源**：netbsd-users 邮件列表 + Adam Glass（NetBSD 核心团队成员） + 1994-12-23 + **<https://mail-index.netbsd.org/netbsd-users/1994/12/23/0000.html>** + 访问日期 2026-07-27
- **新闻摘要**：Adam Glass 代表 NetBSD 核心团队在 netbsd-users 邮件列表发布公告："On December 20, Theo de Raadt was asked to resign from the NetBSD Project by the remaining members of ‘core’. This was a very difficult decision to make, and resulted from Theo's long history of rudeness towards and abuse of users and developers of NetBSD. We believe that there is no place for that type of behaviour from representatives of the NetBSD Project, and that, overall, it has been damaging to the project.“邮件同时承认 Theo 的历史贡献：”He was the principal caretaker of NetBSD's SPARC support, and has written too much code to mention“，并表示”We are certainly willing to accept (and would very much like to see) future contributions from Theo, but we believe that it is inappropriate for him to be an ‘official’ representative of the project any longer"。
- **与本章主题的关联说明**：本章叙事须呈现 OpenBSD fork 的起因，Adam Glass 1994-12-23 邮件是 NetBSD 核心团队对 de Raadt 离开事件的官方公告，与 OpenBSD FAQ 形成 NetBSD 阵营视角的对照。邮件所述“长期对用户与开发者粗鲁与滥用”是 NetBSD 阵营对冲突起因的官方陈述，构成本章“OpenBSD fork 起因”叙事的 NetBSD 一方一手史料。
- **与官方史料的交叉验证结论**：该邮件为 NetBSD 核心团队官方公告，与 OpenBSD FAQ 历史章节形成双方陈述对照。两则来源对事件性质的措辞存在细微差异——OpenBSD FAQ 措辞“forced to resign”暗示压力下的被动辞职，NetBSD 核心团队措辞“asked to resign”暗示 Core Team 主动决定。史家引用时须呈现双方措辞差异而非统一化处理。

### 新闻条目 3

- **新闻标题（中英）**：OpenBSD Project Goals（OpenBSD 项目目标）
- **新闻来源**：OpenBSD Project 官方网站 + OpenBSD 项目维护者集体署名 + 持续维护至 2026 年 + **<https://www.openbsd.org/goals.html>** + 访问日期 2026-07-27
- **新闻摘要**：OpenBSD 项目目标页面明确记载九大目标，包括“Provide the best development platform possible”、"Integrate good code from any source with acceptable licenses. ISC or Berkeley style licences are preferred, the GPL is not acceptable when adding new code“、”Pay attention to security problems and fix them before anyone else does. (Try to be the #1 most secure operating system.)“、”Greater integration of cryptographic software“、”Track and implement standards (ANSI, POSIX, parts of X/Open, etc.)“、”Work towards a very machine independent source tree“、”Be as politics-free as possible; solutions should be decided on the basis of technical merit“、”Focus on being developer-oriented in all senses, including holding developer-only events called hackathons“、”Do not let serious problems sit unsolved“、”Make a release approximately every six months“。页面同时说明”OpenBSD is developed and released from Canada and due to Canadian law it is legal to export crypto to the world"，这是 OpenBSD 选择加拿大作为开发与发布地的法律依据。
- **与本章主题的关联说明**：本章叙事须呈现 OpenBSD“安全至上”定位的官方表述，OpenBSD 项目目标页面是 OpenBSD 项目对自身“安全至上”定位最权威的一手官方史料，明确“Try to be the #1 most secure operating system”作为项目目标，构成本章“安全至上起源”叙事的官方目标陈述。加拿大加密出口法律依据亦解释了 OpenBSD 选择加拿大作为开发地的地缘政治因素。
- **与官方史料的交叉验证结论**：该页面为 OpenBSD 项目官方史料，所述“安全第一”目标与 OpenBSD security.html（故障类条目 2）所述“We have been auditing since the summer of 1996”形成目标与工程实践的双重印证。所述“加拿大法律允许加密出口”与 OpenBSD FAQ 历史章节一致。
