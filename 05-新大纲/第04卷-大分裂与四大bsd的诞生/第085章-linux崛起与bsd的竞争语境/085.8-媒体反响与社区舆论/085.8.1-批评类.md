### 新闻条目 1

- **新闻标题（中英）**：The unknown hackers — Open-source pioneers Bill and Lynne Jolitz may be the most famous programmers you've never heard of（鲜为人知的黑客 —— 开源先驱 Bill 与 Lynne Jolitz 可能是你从未听说过的最著名的程序员）
- **新闻来源**：Salon + Rachel Chalmers + 2000-05-17 + **<https://www.salon.com/2000/05/17/386bsd/>** + 访问日期 2026-07-27
- **新闻摘要**：Rachel Chalmers 在 Salon 长篇专访中记载 386BSD 与 Linux 的关系："Not many Linux-come-latelies know this, but Linux was actually the second open-source Unix-based operating system for personal computers to be distributed over the Internet. The first was 386BSD, which was put together by an extraordinary couple named Bill and Lynne Jolitz.“文章引用 Eric S. Raymond 的判断：”The deciding factor, argues Raymond, was not technological but social. Torvalds, even while practicing rigorous quality control in determining what goes into the Linux kernel and what stays out, nevertheless welcomes contributions and is remarkably generous in sharing credit. The Jolitzes had a very different style.“文章同时记载 BSD 阵营反控：”The xBSDs don't have any understanding of where we were heading,“says Lynne. ”They're looking to the past."
- **与本章主题的关联说明**：本章论战叙事须兼顾 Linux 与 BSD 双方视角，Salon 专访是少数同时引用 Linus Torvalds、Eric S. Raymond、Bill 与 Lynne Jolitz 三方陈述的二手综合文献，为论战叙事提供多视角锚点。文章中"Linux was actually the second open-source Unix-based operating system for personal computers"的判断印证 BSD 阵营对 Linux 后发但反超的叙事核心。
- **与官方史料的交叉验证结论**：Salon 文章关于“386BSD 先于 Linux 发布”的判断与 386BSD Release 0.0 1991-03-17（圣帕特里克日）发布〔存疑订正：386BSD Release 0.0 通行记载为 1992-02-14，1991 年系 Jolitz 在 Dr. Dobb's Journal 连载 Porting UNIX to the 386 的起始年，此处 1991-03-17 疑与连载时间混淆，待回源核〕、Linux 0.01 1991-09-17 发布的时序一致；关于“Linus Torvalds 1993 年 Meta 杂志访谈”的转引经多源印证；关于“Eric Raymond 判断 Linux 与 386BSD 的差异是社会而非技术”的转引须与 Raymond 原文交叉验证（参见新闻条目 3）。
