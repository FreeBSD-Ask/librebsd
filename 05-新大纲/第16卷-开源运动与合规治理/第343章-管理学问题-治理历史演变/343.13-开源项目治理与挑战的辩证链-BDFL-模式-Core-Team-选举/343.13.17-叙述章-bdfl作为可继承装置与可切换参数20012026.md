# 343.13.17 叙述章——BDFL 作为可继承装置与可切换参数（2001–2026）

2001 年 2 月 Christoph Hellwig 在 linux-mm 驳回 Moshe Barr 关于 FreeBSD 比 Linux 2.4 快 30% 的基准说法：“As he has not even documented all the parameters: Ignore him.”，Jean Francois Martinez 称 Linux 存在宣传问题——“the BSD people will mention this for ten years”——Kip Macy 则指出宣传风向本就有利于 Linux，Matt Dillon 披露“I fubar'd heavy load performance for a month in FreeBSD-4.2”——BDFL 作为个人权威工具的第一阶段就此埋下伏笔

2015 至 2016 年，BDFL 顶端之下的评审文化与许可证执行权首次被系统讨论。Geert Uytterhoeven 指出多轮修改后常只剩沉默：“no Acked-by or Reviewed-By. Just silence.”，并警告强制 Reviewed-by 若无真实激励可能反受其害：“may turn out to make matters worse”；David Miller applies NetBSD 维护者 Justin Cormack 的 macvtap 修复：“Signed-off-by: Justin Cormack <justin@netbsd.org> Applied.”——BSD 阵营维护者身份开始进入 Linux 内核治理链路；同期 GPL defense CORE TOPIC 中 Theodore Ts'o 与 Jeremy Allison 就“shiny red button”是否该由 Linus 而非 SFC 按下展开争论：“lots of them work on FreeBSD not Linux”，Luis R. Rodriguez 辩护 Conservancy 立场，Greg KH 直言“when it comes to the kernel actions actually speak more loudly towards advocating permissive licenses than the GPL”

2018 年 9 月 6 日 ksummit-discuss 的 Succession Planning 线程是本节最高潮：James Bottomley 以 Linus 记错峰会档期为由开启接班人计划：“since our fearless leader apparently can't even remember the dates of the only conference he goes to, perhaps now might be a good time to talk about how we'd run an orderly succession process”，并给出本节最具穿透力的一句对照：“I like the single leader model because it doesn't lead to the cabal cult like the group maintainer model does in BSD”；Daniel Vetter 反问“Why I single leader?”，Geert Uytterhoeven 补出反例“Arm-soc has a triumvirate doing round-roll releases/pull-requests”，Olof Johansson 主张“having a group would be substantially more robust”，Linus 回应“trust within the group is I think the most important part by far”并以自嘲收尾“what better opportunity for a palace coup than when the old dictator is off gallivanting?”——BDFL 顶端裁决权与集体维护模式的辩证在此被首次系统讨论

同月 Linus Walleij 治理心理学长文指出，维护者在外部仍被当作“superior beings”，兼具耐心与批评比例（80% 表扬 20% 批评）与工程能力者交集极小——这是继承风险的根源；他以 David Brownell 之死作答：GPIO 长期无人维护，补丁只能由 Andrew Morton 按默认路径合入，湍流期合入的 GPIO sysfs 接口把全局 GPIO numberspace 暴露成半 ABI、维护噩梦至今；对比 MMC 能找到前任维护者 Pierre Ossman 解开 bounce buffer 之谜——BDFL 继承风险被量化为具体技术遗产

2019 年 9 月 TAB 选举改用 Condorcet Internet Voting Service，Matthew Wilcox 表态：“Condorcet is a superior system and allowing people who can't make it to the conference to vote is beneficial for the legitimacy of the TAB”，CIVS 源代码以 BSD-like 研究许可证发布——BSD 许可证体系与 Linux 治理机构 TAB 选举在此交汇；同期 Konstantin Ryabitsev 提议 public-inbox 客户端作为 Patchwork 替代：“what we envision can be described as "local patchwork with a mutt-like interface"”，Linus 表态“So I don't think we can force some workflows”——BDFL 治理从邮件列表基础设施向新协作平台演化

2020 至 2023 年，BSD 阵营以具体技术与身份方式渗透 Linux 治理。Bottomley 引 BSDCan 注册页为 LSFMMBPF 议题取消的票价基准：“I was basing that on https://www.bsdcan.org/2020/registration.php”；Ingo Schwarze 的“OpenBSD committer and mandoc(1) maintainer”身份被写入 Linux mount_setattr 手册页规范论证；Linus 在与 Colomar 的 ISO C 命名之争中反问“Do you really want to impose your own aesthetic on the maintainers and then argue with him when he disagrees?”；FreeBSD coredump 被引为 Linux PT_NOTE p_align=4 的先例：“FreeBSD coredumps set p_align to 4 as well.”

2023 年 10 月至 2024 年 2 月 Theo de Raadt 以 deraad@openbsd.org 直接参与 mseal syscall RFC，坚持“Unix system calls must be atomic.”，Linus 采纳命名建议“I do like us starting with just mimmutable(), since it already exists.”——mimmutable 谱系直接来自 OpenBSD；2025 年 2 月 Jakub Kicinski 提交“MAINTAINERS: add a general entry for BSD sockets”，Patchwork bot 确认落入 netdev/net.git 主分支，Paolo Abeni 补充“We are working to increase review coverage over the net code-base tweaking the MAINTAINER entries”——BSD sockets 接口在 MAINTAINERS 中获得独立条目

2025 年 8 月 Paul McKenney 把 AI 辅助代码标注提交维护者峰会，Jiri Kosina 认为法务层面可由基金会与 DCO 承接，核心是要说明谁真正理解代码：“if I need to talk to a human that actually does understand the patch deeply”；2026 年 1 月 Dan Williams 提交项目连续性文档，自注“a follow-up from Maintainer Summit”，内容是“a plan for a plan for navigating events that affect the forward progress of the canonical Linux repository, torvalds/linux.git”；2026 年 8 月 Dave Airlie 把维护者峰会描述为“Linus's staff meeting”，Theodore Ts'o 说明其为约 30 人半天流程议题会议，Geert 追问“Forgive my ignorance, how do the sponsored attendees fit into this picture?”——BDFL 顶端与基金会-维护者-赞助方的三层关系首次在议题上被摆出

BDFL 模式在此完成从个人权威到可继承装置的转化：顶端裁决权被 TAB、Condorcet、project continuity 三层装置稀释，但未被取代；继承、评审、选举、连续性与工具链五层问题被拆开分别处置；BDFL 与 BSD 集体维护的辩证在 Linux 现场未被二选一，而是被吸收为治理哲学的可切换参数——当维护者更替时切换至集体模式，当技术决策需要快速收敛时切换至单一模式，BDFL 成为可继承的治理装置而非不可复制的个人权威；从 2001 年 Christoph Hellwig 对 FreeBSD 基准的驳回到 2026 年 Dan Williams 的项目连续性文档，BDFL 在二十五年的时间线上完成了从个人权威工具到制度化治理装置的三级跨越
