▶ Virtualized timer

(interrupts and timer)
▶ Platforms:
▶ Emulator from FastModels (Cortex A15)
▶ Cubieboard2
▶ More tech details about interrupt controller virtualization on
AsiaBSDCon2018 presentation (11th of March 2018)
▶ Timer virtualization was ment for BSDCan2018 but not
accepted
