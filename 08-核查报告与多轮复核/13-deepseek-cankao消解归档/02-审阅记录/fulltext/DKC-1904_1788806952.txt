BSD Hypervisor 
 About bhyve
 bhyve, the "BSD hypervisor", pronounced "beehive" is a hypervisor/virtual machine manager available on FreeBSD and illumos. The weekly Production Users conference call minutes [https://docs.google.com/document/d/1PFUmz6XpTVAGkq5dBe8uaBFV2Y4i-uR88AuiCLIRxIQ/edit] and Production User call recordings [https://www.youtube.com/@bhyvecon] are available online and all are welcome to join.
 bhyvecon [https://bhyvecon.org] is the only conference dedicated to BSD Hypervisors including FreeBSD/Illumos bhyve, FreeBSD/NetBSD Xen, OpenBSD vmm, and NetBSD Xen/nvmm/HAXM.
