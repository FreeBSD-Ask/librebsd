## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

1980 年 DARPA 资助标准 UNIX 平台（5.4／5.5 核心句；原文“development”语法瑕疵照录）：

> In 1980, based on the success of the 3BSD operating system, the Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (DARPA) agreed to provide UC Berkeley with funding to development a standard UNIX platform for future DARPA research.

阶段序列的档案实体（5.4.6 编年支撑）：

> a vast quantity of magnetic data reels and CD-ROMs containing complete distributions of the operating system from various stages in its development.

UCB—AT&T 合作的法律测试场（5.5 许可面）：

> As a product of cooperation between the University of California, Berkeley, and AT&T, BSD served as a testing ground for the legal implications of university/corporate relationships in computer software development.

3BSD 阶段与 AT&T 源码依赖（5.4 阶段划分）：

> the next release, 3BSD was a full-fledged operating system, although it still depended heavily on AT&T's proprietary UNIX source code.
