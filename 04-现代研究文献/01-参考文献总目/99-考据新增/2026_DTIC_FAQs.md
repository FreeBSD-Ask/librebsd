## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

提交义务：

> These regulations: Assign DoD components the responsibility for ensuring that all pertinent material resulting from Research, Development, Test, and Evaluation (RDT&E) programs is provided to DTIC.

> Yes. DoD regulations require DoD organizations to submit their content to DTIC.

馆藏对象：

> DTIC collects scientific and technical documents either created by DoD/military components or funded by DoD/military components, whole or in part, through grants, contracts, or MIPRs.

分发声明（开放门槛）：

> All documents submitted to DTIC require a Distribution Statement or must be cleared for public release. This statement specifies who is authorized to have access to your document.

## 三、考据说明

1. FAQ 明列规范依据：DoD Instruction 3200.12、DoD Manual 3200.14 Vol.1、DFARS 235.010、DFARS 252.235-7011——提交义务来自国防部规章，不是机构自愿。
2. 馆藏对象含“经 grants、contracts 或 MIPRs 全部或部分资助”的文献，正是 ARPA／DARPA 类资助产出的落点。
3. 分发声明制度是开放与否的闸门：同一馆藏内按声明分级，故“开放情况”须逐件核声明、并注访问日。
4. 禁引核查：`.mil` 官方站，`banned_source=0`。
