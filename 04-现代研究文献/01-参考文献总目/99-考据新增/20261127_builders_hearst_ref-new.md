## 逐字摘录（正文）

> In 1891, Hearst established scholarships for women at the University of California. She later funded the Phoebe Apperson Hearst International Architectural Competition to develop a master plan for the young institution. This plan produced many of the campus's most notable buildings, including the Hearst Memorial Mining Building, which she had built as a memorial to her husband.

> When she became the first woman Regent in 1897, she had a building erected adjacent to her residence near the campus where she hosted students. The building, known as Hearst Hall, was later moved to campus and became the university's first gymnasium and social center for women.
