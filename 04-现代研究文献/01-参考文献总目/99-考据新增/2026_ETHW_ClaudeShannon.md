## 正文摘录（逐字）

"He developed the mathematical theories and techniques that make possible the analysis of switching circuits, computers and communications."

"In a master’s thesis which has proven critically important, he demonstrated that Boolean logic could be worked with electrical switching circuits."

"This thesis was meant to help the telephone industry move from human operators to complex switching circuits."

## 提要

ETHW 记：香农发展出使开关电路、计算机与通信之分析成为可能的数学理论与技术；在一篇后来被证明极为关键的硕士论文中，他展示布尔逻辑可用电气开关电路来实现；该论文意在协助电话业由人工接线转向复杂交换电路。该页亦未载论文年份。
