## 正文摘录（逐字）

"Aiken's first calculator, the Mark I, was assembled in February 1944, and ushered in the digital computing age."

"The machine was presented by the International Business Machines Corporation to Harvard University in 1944."

"The Mark I generated immediate interest of the United States Navy."

"The Mark I was used in gunnery, ballistics, and naval design by the Navy Bureau of Ships until January 1946, at which time it was transferred to the Navy Board of Ordnance."

"Aiken continued to work at Harvard until 1961, building up to the Mark IV."

## 提要

ETHW 记：艾肯首台计算机马克一号于 1944 年 2 月装配完成，开数字计算时代；同年由 IBM 赠予哈佛大学；迅即引起美国海军兴趣，由舰船局用于火炮、弹道与舰船设计，至 1946 年 1 月移交海军军械局；艾肯在哈佛工作至 1961 年，机型发展至 Mark IV。
