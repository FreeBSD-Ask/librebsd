## 正文摘录（逐字）

"It was generally believed that the first electronic digital computers were the Colossus, built in England in 1943, and the ENIAC, built in the United States in 1945."

"However, the first special-purpose electronic computer may actually have been invented by John Vincent Atanasoff, a physicist and mathematician at Iowa State College (now Iowa State University), during 1937–42."

"Together with his graduate assistant Clifford E. Berry, Atanasoff built a successful small prototype in 1939 for the purpose of testing two ideas central to his design: capacitors to store data in binary form and electronic logic circuits to perform addition and subtraction."

"Various components of the ABC were designed and built from 1939 to 1942, but development was discontinued with the onset of World War II."

"In 1973 Judge Earl Larson voided the ENIAC patent on the grounds that the ABC was the first electronic digital computer and that Mauchly had not considered such a device until his time with Atanasoff."

## 提要

Britannica 记：人们一般相信最早的电子数字计算机是 1943 年英国建造的 Colossus 与 1945 年美国建造的 ENIAC；但第一台专用电子计算机可能实为艾奥瓦州立学院物理与数学教授 Atanasoff 在 1937—1942 年间发明。Atanasoff 与研究生 Berry 于 1939 年建成小型原型，用以验证其设计的两项核心构想——以电容按二进制存储数据、以电子逻辑电路做加减；随后设计建造更通用的 ABC，各部件 1939—1942 年间陆续制成，因二战爆发而中止。该条并记：1973 年法官 Earl Larson 判定 ENIAC 专利无效，理由为 ABC 是第一台电子数字计算机，且 Mauchly 在拜访 Atanasoff 之前未曾考虑此类装置。
