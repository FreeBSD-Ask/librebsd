## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

设立依据与职能（227.1 Administrative History）：

> Established: In the Office for Emergency Management (OEM) by EO 8807, June 28, 1941.

> Functions: Advised the President on status of scientific and medical research relating to national defense. Coordinated federal government research relating to national defense.

合同资助大学实验室（227.10 及其下条目）：

> 227.10 Records of Laboratories under Contract with OSRD 1940-46

> History: Established to research and develop microwave radar defense systems by Contract NDCrc 53, concluded between NDRC and MIT, February 5, 1941. Continued under Contract OEMsr 262, concluded between OSRD and MIT, February 17, 1942.

## 三、说明

记录组指南确认 OSRD 于 1941 年 6 月 28 日依第 8807 号行政命令设立，职能为就国防相关科研向总统提供建议并协调联邦研究；其下设“与 OSRD 签约的实验室”类档案，MIT 雷达实验室即以合同方式承接（1941 年 NDRC 合同、1942 年 OSRD 续约）。属官方档案指南记载。
