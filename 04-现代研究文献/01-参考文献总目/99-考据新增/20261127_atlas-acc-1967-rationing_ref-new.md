## 引文摘录

> At present no charge was made to university users, but rationing by money was one method of allocating time. Unless this was followed rationing would have to be by merit, and merit in the work's own discipline rather than in terms of its interest as a computer problem.

上下文：会议第 2.1 项“The Work Load”讨论实验室过载时的优先级分配；记录接着写道，按 merit 配给“would be a difficult and invidious job for the Director or even for the Computer Committee”，并认为按 merit 配给等同于授予机时，与拨款性质相近。

## 用途

支撑 V03.3.4.6：机时配给在“按经费”与“按学科择优”之间取舍，委员会自承后者困难且易招怨，说明配额规则是价值判断而非中立技术程序。
