## 正文摘录（逐字）

"The semiconductors now in use are artificial products made from elements such as germanium or silicon."

"The pure element has very few carriers. Through small additions of certain contaminants, however, it is possible to alter the supply of carriers."

"Every atom of phosphorus, forced as a lodger on silicon, donates one carrier to the house, a negative electron."

"A few parts in 100,000 make a good semiconductor."

"Still more remarkable is that a guest atom of boron provides a carrier of the opposite kind – positive."

"This idea was put forward in the 1930's, at a time when rectifiers based on semiconductors began to find important uses."

## 提要

Rudberg 的颁奖词以导体／绝缘体／半导体三类载流子密度作对比，指出当时使用的半导体是锗、硅等元素制成的人造产品；纯净元素载流子极少，而少量掺入特定杂质即可改变载流子供给——磷原子在硅中充当施主贡献一个负电子，十万分之几即构成良半导体；硼原子则提供相反的正载流子。颁奖词并指出，两类载流子的观念在 1930 年代、半导体整流器开始重要应用时提出。

## 存疑与边界

- 颁奖词为 1956 年文本，其“当时使用的半导体”指锗、硅；不含区熔提纯工艺，提纯侧由 Pfann 1952 承担。
- “A few parts in 100,000”为颁奖词的量级表述，非精确工艺参数。
