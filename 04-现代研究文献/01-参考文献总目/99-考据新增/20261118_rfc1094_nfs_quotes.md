## 逐字引文

Q1（2.2.3 A 源，1. INTRODUCTION 首段）:
The Sun Network Filesystem (NFS) protocol provides transparent remote access to shared files across networks. The NFS protocol is designed to be portable across different machines, operating systems, network architectures, and transport protocols.
