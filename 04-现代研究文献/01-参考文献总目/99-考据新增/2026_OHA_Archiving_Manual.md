## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

口述访谈的性质：

> While considering an archival plan for oral history interviews, it will be helpful to consider the multifaceted nature of oral history. Oral history interviews are: primary sources that are meant to be accessible, discoverable, and understood; evidence for historical and anthropological understandings, as well as other disciplines; cultural objects; and archival objects requiring long-term preservation.

入藏的定义：

> Responsible accessioning is the first step by which a repository gains physical and intellectual control over an interview or collection.

入藏时须登记元数据：

> Ensure there is sufficient and consistent metadata collected at the time of donation so that content, context, and provenance are recorded, even if time passes between acquisition and full archival processing.

鉴定（appraisal）的两步：

> Overview: The arrival of interviews at a repository is one of the most critical junctures in an interview’s life cycle. There are two steps in this process: appraisal (the process of determining which interviews should be accepted by a repository) and accessioning (the actions that archivists take to acquire legal and physical custody of the interviews).

## 三、考据说明

1. 手册把口述访谈定性为“一手史料＋跨学科证据＋文化物件＋需长期保存的档案物件”，四重性质决定其存世与可用面不同于普通文书。
2. 入藏＝取得实物与知识控制权，并在捐赠时即登记内容、语境与来源（provenance）元数据；延迟处理不构成延迟登记的理由。
3. 鉴定与入藏分两步：先判收与不收，再取法律与实物保管权——这为“回忆材料的存世”提供了可核查的制度环节。
