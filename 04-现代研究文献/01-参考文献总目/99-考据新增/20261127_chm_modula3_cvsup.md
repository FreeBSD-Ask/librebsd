- **quote（逐字，两段）**：

  > For a number of years, FreeBSD development was based on the CVS revision control system, using the program CVSup to replicate CVS repositories. CVSup was written in Modula-3, and its author, John D. Polstra, maintained the EZM3 distribution of Modula-3.

  > Ezm3 is a simpler, more portable distribution of the Modula-3 compiler and runtime libraries. It is designed for people who would like to install CVSup on their systems as quickly and as painlessly as possible. Ezm3 is derived from the robust and comprehensive PM3 release of Modula-3. Only the components and features which are needed by CVSup were retained.
