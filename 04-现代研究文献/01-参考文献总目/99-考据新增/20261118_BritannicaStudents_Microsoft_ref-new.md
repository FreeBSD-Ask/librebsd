## b007 引文（2026-11-18 逐字录入）

Microsoft was founded in 1975 by Bill Gates and Paul G. Allen, two boyhood friends from Seattle. Earlier that year they had converted BASIC, a popular mainframe computer programming language, for use on an early personal computer (PC), the Altair. Shortly afterward, they established Microsoft, deriving the name from the words microcomputer and software.
