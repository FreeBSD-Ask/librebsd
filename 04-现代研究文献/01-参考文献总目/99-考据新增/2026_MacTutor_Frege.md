## 正文摘录（逐字）

"In 1879 Frege published his first major work Begriffsschrift, eine der arithmetischen nachgebildete Formelsprache des reinen Denkens (Concept notation, an arithmetic formula modeled language of pure thought)."

"In 1893 Die Grundgesetze der Arithmetik, Volume 1 (The basic laws of arithmetic, Volume 1) appeared in which Frege set up a formal logical system with more rules of inference than that of his earlier work the Begriffsschrift."

"While Volume 2 of The Basic Laws of Arithmetic was at the printers Frege received a letter (on 16 June 1902) from Bertrand Russell."

"Russell pointed out, with great modesty, that the Russell paradox gave a contradiction in Frege's system of axioms."

"One often sees it stated that Frege's work was worthless because of the inconsistency pointed out by Russell."

## 提要

MacTutor 记：弗雷格 1879 年刊《概念文字》，1893 年刊《算术基本法则》第一卷并建立形式逻辑系统；第二卷付印期间于 1902 年 6 月 16 日收到罗素来信，罗素指出罗素悖论使其公理系统出现矛盾；后世常误传弗雷格工作因此报废。该页亦载其工作长期少人识。
