## 正文摘录（逐字）

"In 1936 he published On Computable Numbers, with an application to the Entscheidungsproblem."

"He defined a computable number as real number whose decimal expansion could be produced by a Turing machine starting with a blank tape."

"He showed that π was computable, but since only countably many real numbers are computable, most real numbers are not computable."

"Turing's revised paper contains a reference to Church's results and the paper, first completed in April 1936, was revised in this way in August 1936 and it appeared in print in 1937."

## 提要

MacTutor 记：图灵 1936 年发表《论可计算数及其在判定问题上的应用》；把可计算数定义为可由图灵机自空白带产出其十进制展开的实数；证明 π 可算，但因可算实数仅可数多个，故大多数实数不可算；论文 1936 年 4 月完成，8 月据丘奇结果修订，1937 年印行。
