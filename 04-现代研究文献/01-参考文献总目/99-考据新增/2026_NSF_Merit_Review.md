## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

> Typically, NSF program officers identify external reviewers, who are experts in the fields represented by the proposal, to review the proposal. The review may be conducted by ad hoc reviewers, a panel of experts, or a combination of both.

> Reviewers evaluate the proposal using the two National Science Board-approved merit review criteria: intellectual merit and broader impacts. These criteria cover both the quality of the research and the project's potential impact on society.

> The potential for the proposed project to advance knowledge and understanding within its own field or across different fields.

> The potential for the proposed project to benefit society and contribute to the achievement of specific, desired societal outcomes.

## 三、说明

NSF 官方说明其资助决定机制：项目官员初检后择定外部评审人（ad hoc 评审、专家组或二者兼用），按国家科学委员会批准的两项标准——知识价值（intellectual merit）与广泛影响（broader impacts）——评审，再结合其他因素形成资助组合。此与 42 U.S.C. § 1862 的法定授权互证，勾勒“法定渠道＋同行评议”的资助架构。
