## 摘要摘录（逐字，Crossref 官方元数据 abstract 字段）

"the energy spectrum of an electron moving in a perfect lattice splits up into bands of allowed and disallowed energies, and if there are just sufficient electrons present to fill up one of the allowed bands there can be no conductivity at absolute zero temperature."

"Under these conditions it was shown that if ΔW is the minimum energy required to remove an electron into the next higher band of allowed energies, then for low temperatures the conductivity σ is given by σ = σ0 exp (−ΔW/2kT)."

"If this is true, then no pure non-metallic solid can ever have a significant natural electronic conductivity at ordinary temperatures, and the observed conductivity of semi-conductors must be due to the presence of impurities."

"there are substances such as silicon which show a negative temperature coefficient in the impure state, but which are good metallic conductors in the pure state and are therefore to be classed as metals."

## 提要

Wilson 第二篇论文摘要称：完美晶格中运动电子的能谱分裂为允许带与禁带；若电子恰好填满某个允许带，绝对零度下不导电。由此给出低温电导率随 ΔW 指数变化的公式 σ = σ0 exp(−ΔW/2kT)。摘要进一步推论：若 ΔW 达数伏，纯净的非金属固体在常温下不可能有显著的本征电子导电，半导体的实测电导必须归因于杂质的存在。摘要并举硅为例：不纯时呈负温度系数，纯净时却是良金属导体，故应归入金属类。

## 存疑与边界

- 摘要中 ΔW 的数值（氧化亚铜约 0.3 V，纯物质约 2 V）属实验对照，本批块内不引具体数值，避免超出摘要可核范围。
