## 正文摘录（逐字）

"Although Multics is now offered as a standard product by Honeywell Information Systems Inc., the system planning and development was a cooperative effort involving three separate organizations: the computer department of the General Electric Company (since acquired by Honeywell Information Systems Inc.) the Bell Telephone Laboratories (1965 to 1969) and Project MAC of M.I.T. (since renamed the M.I.T. Laboratory for Computer Science and then the Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory)."

"In the case of the Multics project the three organizations attached different levels of importance to 1) research and development in computer science, 2) development of a commercial product, and 3) useful computational service in the short term. The inclusion of several objectives in turn left unresolved priorities of time, money, or concept demonstration. The default effect was to favor concept demonstration but not without great stress because of the unplanned delay which resulted."

## 提要

Corbató 与 Clingen 追述：Multics 现已是 Honeywell 的标准产品，而其规划与研制原是 GE 计算机部门、贝尔实验室（1965–1969）与 MIT Project MAC 三方合作项目；三方对目标重要性排序各异——计算机科学研发、商业产品、短期计算服务——多重目标使时间、经费与概念验证的优先级悬而未决，默认偏向概念演示，代价是未预期的延期与巨大压力。
