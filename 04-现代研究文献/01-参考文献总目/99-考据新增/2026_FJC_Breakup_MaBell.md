## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

> United States v. AT&T was dismissed on August 24, 1982, and the divestment of AT&T’s local operating companies was implemented on January 1, 1984. Those companies, which were consolidated into seven regional operating companies (nicknamed “Baby Bells”), were permitted to keep the Bell name as a result of a decision by Judge Greene.

> Above all else, AT&T’s management did not want to risk losing Western Electric, which it saw as a crucial part of the company’s future, together with Bell Labs.

## 三、说明

FJC 记述：1982-08-24 案件撤诉，1984-01-01 实施本地运营公司剥离，22 家运营公司合并为七家地区运营公司（俗称 Baby Bells）；AT&T 管理层最不愿失去西电与贝尔实验室——印证西电（制造主体）未被剥离，与判决令文互证，可纠正“制造主体剥离”的通俗表述。
