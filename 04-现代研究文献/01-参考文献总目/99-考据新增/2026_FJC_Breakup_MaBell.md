## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

> United States v. AT&T was dismissed on August 24, 1982, and the divestment of AT&T’s local operating companies was implemented on January 1, 1984. Those companies, which were consolidated into seven regional operating companies (nicknamed “Baby Bells”), were permitted to keep the Bell name as a result of a decision by Judge Greene.

> Above all else, AT&T’s management did not want to risk losing Western Electric, which it saw as a crucial part of the company’s future, together with Bell Labs.

2026-11-17 追加（b015；FJC 直连被 WAF 阻断，exa 文本层取文，引文逐字录入）：

1956 年和解改采非结构救济（2.2.4 用）：

> The government sought to force AT&T to divest its interest in Western, but the parties settled the case in 1956 with a far less drastic remedy. The consent decree AT&T signed included no divestiture of assets; the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) had informed DOJ that it did not deem divestiture of Western necessary.

1974 年起诉的政治背景（2.2.6 用）：

> William Saxbe, who had been appointed attorney general of the United States in January 1974, was anxious to restore public confidence in the DOJ, which was at a low ebb after the Watergate scandal. When the Antitrust Division recommended filing suit against AT&T, Saxbe was eager to proceed, and the suit was filed in the U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia on November 20, 1974.

## 三、说明

FJC 记述：1982-08-24 案件撤诉，1984-01-01 实施本地运营公司剥离，22 家运营公司合并为七家地区运营公司（俗称 Baby Bells）；AT&T 管理层最不愿失去西电与贝尔实验室——印证西电（制造主体）未被剥离，与判决令文互证，可纠正“制造主体剥离”的通俗表述。
