## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

版本号对齐上游：

> No, you didn’t miss a release; we simply re-numbered the 13.1 release to 13.3 to align with its updated FreeBSD 13.3 operating system!

发版计划由厂商宣告（b012 补录）：

> Today, we are announcing our plans to release TrueNAS CORE 13.3 in the next few months.

商业赞助与公开流程（b014 补录）：

> At iXsystems, we have worked hard for many years to be the best possible corporate sponsors for open-source projects. Unlike proprietary vendors, our processes and planning are done in the open, and both community members and customers alike play an important role in how TrueNAS continues to evolve.

两版并存：

> Both TrueNAS CORE and TrueNAS SCALE will exist to address the needs of different users. The focus of TrueNAS CORE continues to be ensuring storage reliability, stability, and security for existing users.

sidegrade 路径：

> TrueNAS CORE users will always have the ability to “sidegrade” to SCALE if and when they’re ready.

新功能在 SCALE 主线：

> TrueNAS SCALE is the software edition where new features and updated components are actively developed and tested.

CORE 的支持承诺：

> It is not anywhere near its end-of-lifecycle phase. We are just going through a new release cycle for CORE and users can expect to receive maintenance updates for many years still to come.

未废弃的引述：

> “TrueNAS CORE hasn’t been deprecated, and [13.3] is planned to start making a showing in Q2. It will be based upon FreeBSD 13.3 and will provide a way to keep running jails and upstream packages for some time to come. It is still a rock-solid NAS and we’re expecting to support it for a long while for that use-case.”

## 三、考据说明

- “re-numbered the 13.1 release to 13.3 to align with its updated FreeBSD 13.3 operating system”说明版本号随上游 FreeBSD 版本走（2.6.1-B）。
- “Both TrueNAS CORE and TrueNAS SCALE will exist…”是 2.6.2 品牌结构后期形态（CORE＋SCALE 两版并存）。
- “sidegrade”句是 2.7.4 的迁移路径第二来源。
- “hasn’t been deprecated”句系博客**引述社区论坛帖**，claim 必须写明“引述”，不得当作厂商正式声明。
