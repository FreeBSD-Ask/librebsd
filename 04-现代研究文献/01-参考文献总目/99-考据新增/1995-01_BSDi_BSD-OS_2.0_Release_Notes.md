## Introduction

Welcome to Release 2.0 of BSD/OS. This system is based on the 4.4BSD-Lite release from the Computer Systems Research Group at the University of California at Berkeley. We hope it meets your requirements.

## Problem Resolution

BSD/OS includes 60 days of full telephone support from the day you receive the initial shipment.

Customers can purchase additional support in the form of:

• Upgrades only

• Upgrades + PaperMail/e-mail/fax support

• Upgrades + PaperMail/e-mail/fax and phone support

• By-the-minute telephone support

## What's New in BSD/OS 2.0

### Kernel

• 4.4BSD kernel source base

• Dramatic improvement in disk I/O performance

• 64-bit file size

• Security improvements to thwart TCP/IP impersonators

• FFS: short symlinks in inode, file type kept in directory, clustered I/O, super precise timestamps, no reboot after fsck changes root, NQNFS with cache consistency

## Update Notes from VI.1 to V2.0

Users of the BSD/OS VI.1 should consult the chapter Update Notes from V1.1 to V2.0 for upgrade instructions.
