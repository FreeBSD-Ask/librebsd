## 正文摘录（逐字）

"Prior to the invention of the bipolar transistor in 1947, semiconductors were used only as two-terminal devices, such as rectifiers and photodiodes."

"During the early 1950s germanium was the major semiconductor material."

"Since the early 1960s silicon has become by far the most widely used semiconductor, with a market share of about 98 percent."

"Solid-state materials are commonly grouped into three classes: insulators, semiconductors, and conductors."

## 提要

Britannica 半导体条目记：1947 年双极晶体管发明之前，半导体只用作两端器件，如整流器与光电二极管；1950 年代前期锗是主要半导体材料；1960 年代初起硅成为遥遥领先的最常用半导体，市场份额约 98%。条目并给出三类固态材料的划分与能带形成的一般描述：大量原子结合成晶体时，原子间相互作用使离散能级扩展为能带。
