## 逐字引文池

- The addition of a native threads implementation for all platforms and symmetric multiprocessing (SMP) on i386 and other popular platforms were long-standing goals for NetBSD 2.0.
- SMP support has been added for i386, SPARC, and PowerPC, the SMP support on Alpha and VAX has been improved, and the new port to the 64-bit AMD/Opteron also supports SMP.
- Native thread support has been added, based on Scheduler Activations.
- The i386 port now supports SMP and has a new ACPI and power management framework which takes advantage of Intel's ACPI implementation.
