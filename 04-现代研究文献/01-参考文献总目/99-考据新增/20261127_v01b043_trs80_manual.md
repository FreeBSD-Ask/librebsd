## 正文摘录（逐字）

"An application program seldom talks to the hardware directly. It talks through a disk operating system."

"The Model 4 s disk operating system is TRSDOS Version 6.2 (or any later version)."

"TRSDOS is on the diskette that comes with your Model 4."

## 提要

Tandy 1984 年《TRS-80 Model 4 磁盘系统入门》记：应用程序很少直接与硬件对话，而是通过磁盘操作系统；Model 4 的磁盘操作系统是 TRSDOS 6.2 或更高版本；TRSDOS 就在随机附带的软盘上。三条把“系统随机附送、经零售整机到户”的形态写明，是 4.5.3 的厂商同期依据。
