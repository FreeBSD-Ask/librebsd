## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

> Interstate Commerce Commission (regulation of telephone, telegraph, and cable companies, 1910-34)

> Regulatory responsibility for telegraph, telephone, and cable companies engaged in interstate operations vested in the ICC by the Mann-Elkins (Interstate Commerce) Act, June 18, 1910 (36 Stat. 544), subsequently modified by EO 3513, July 9, 1921, which transferred to the Department of State the responsibility for advising the President on the granting of licenses to submarine cable operators. ICC functions transferred to FCC by the Communications Act of 1934.

## 三、说明

NARA 以档案沿革明确：电话、电报与电缆公司的州际业务监管权，由 1910 年 6 月 18 日《曼恩—埃尔金斯（州际商业）法》（36 Stat. 544）授予州际商业委员会（ICC），ICC 该职能于 1934 年《通信法》施行后移交联邦通信委员会（FCC）。这为“ICC 管辖划定合法边界、后由 FCC 接续”的制度层次提供了档案依据。
