## 正文摘录（逐字）

"EDSAC ran its first successful program on 6th May 1949, making it the world's first fully functional stored-program computer."

"EDSAC was the first computer in the world to be fully operational and practical for general use."

"An earlier stored-program computer known as 'the Manchester Baby' was built at the University of Manchester 11 months before EDSAC, but it was experimental rather than a practical scientific tool."

"The EDSAC was a huge machine, which filled a whole room at the University's Mathematical Laboratory, a precursor of the Department of Computer Science and Technology."

"At the time the Mathematical Laboratory existed to support the University's scientific and technical departments by providing computation and calculation."

"In September 1945 Maurice Wilkes was on his way home from war service, having worked in radar and electronics throughout the Second World War. He was appointed Head of the Mathematical Laboratory in Cambridge, and his remit was to establish a department that could provide computing expertise to the University and support its scientific research."

"EDSAC operated for 35 hours a week, for nine years, and was housed in University buildings in central Cambridge."

## 提要

剑桥大学计算机科学与技术系记：EDSAC 于 1949 年 5 月 6 日首次成功运行程序，是世界上第一台完全可用、可供一般使用的存储程序计算机；早于它 11 个月的曼彻斯特“Baby”属实验机，非实用科学工具。EDSAC 占满数学实验室一整间房，该实验室是计算机科学与技术系的前身，当时以提供计算与制表服务支撑全校理工科院系。1945 年 9 月 Wilkes 自战时雷达与电子工作归国，出任数学实验室主任，其职责是建立一个能为全校提供计算专长、支撑科研的部门。EDSAC 每周运行 35 小时、连续服务九年，机房设在剑桥市中心校舍内。

## 存疑与边界

- 系方用“world's first fully functional stored-program computer”的限定表述，与曼大“world's first stored-program electronic digital computer”并存；两说分属“完全可用／实用”与“首台存储程序电子数字”两个判定面，块内各存原表述。
- 页面未给 EDSAC 的内存字数与首条程序内容。
