## 正文摘录（逐字，Internet Archive 元数据页摘要）

> This historical evaluation of the Advanced Research Projects Agency (ARPA) as an R&D management institution was commissioned by ARPA in recognition of the fact that remarkably little in the way of an official record or institutional memory had been established during its seventeen year lifetime.

> From Agency Directors to program managers, the turnover in its leadership has been rapid by most bureaucratic standards, thus eroding first hand knowledge of ARPA's role and activities rather quickly.

> Conceived as a unique management organization chartered to concentrate on advanced research within the Department of Defense, this very uniqueness has frequently been questioned.

> For the most part, the study ends in 1972 when ARPA was designated a Defense Agency.

## 提要

受 ARPA 委托的“历史评估”：因机关档案与机构记忆留存极少，报告以 ARPA 档案与当事人访谈写就；ARPA 被描述为“以集中研究先进课题为章程的独特管理机构”，其独特性屡遭追问；领导层（自署长至项目经理）轮替之快，使一手知识迅速流失；叙事止于 1972 年 ARPA 改设为国防局。
