## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

NSF 资助 CSNET（1980—1986）：

> In the period from 1980 to 1986, NSF supported the development of CSNET, a "logical network" for computer science researchers.

1985 年连接逾 170 家机构：

> By 1985, CSNET had links to over 170 university, industrial, and government research organizations and numerous gateways to networks in other countries.

合并与由 CREN 续办、会费覆盖成本：

> In 1987 CSNET merged with BITNET, a network serving users from academic institutions that was initiated in 1980-1981.

> CSNET operations were continued under the Corporation for Research and Education Networking, whose operating costs were completely covered by member organizations' dues.

使命完成、1991 秋终止：

> Its mission apparently accomplished, CSNET service was discontinued in the fall of 1991.
