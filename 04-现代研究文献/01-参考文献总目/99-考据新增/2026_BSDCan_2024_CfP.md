## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

技术偏向：

> The talks will have a very strong technical content bias.

商业推销的排除：

> Proposals of a business development or marketing nature are not appropriate for this venue.

讲者注册费：

> Speakers must register but do not pay conference fees. Tutorials not included.

差旅与住宿资助：

> We usually have funds for your flight and accommodation. You pay for your own food and drink.

录制条件（讲台机不可用）：

> In order for recording to occur, you must not use the computer located within the podium.

> use your own laptop, not the venue-supplied computer

## 三、核对记录

- 五条表述与 2025 年征稿页同构（措辞几乎一致），仅录制条 2024 版给出更明确的双句表述（禁用讲台机＋用自带笔记本）。
- 录制条为“未用讲台机即可录制”的工程安排，属条件约定而非成文政策条款。
