## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

分时思想的制度来源（3.3.4 两委员会与后续规划）：

> Much of the time-sharing philosophy, expressed in this paper, has been developed in conjunction with the work of an MIT preliminary study committee, chaired by H. Teager, which examined the long range computational needs of the Institute, and a subsequent MIT computer working committee, chaired by J. McCarthy.

分时的动机（3.3.2）：

> The motivation for time-shared computer usage arises out of the slow man-computer interaction rate presently possible with the bigger, more advanced computers.

工程载体（3.3.1 计算中心设施）：

> The 7090 of the MIT Computation Center has, in addition to three channels with 19 tape units, a fourth channel with the standard Direct Data Connection.

系统规模与形态（3.3.2／3.3.6）：

> In the present system the time-sharing occurs between four users, three of whom are on-line each at a typewriter in a foreground system, and a fourth passive user of the back-ground Fap-Mad-Madtran-BSS Monitor system similar to the Fortran-Fap-BSS Monitor System (FMS) used by most of the Center programmers and by many other 7090 installations.

## 三、考据说明

1. 本文是 CTSS 前身（709／7090 实验分时系统）的同期技术报告，可与 1961 年 11 月 CTSS 演示（库内 CL-RM-000513）区分：本文所述为四人分时实验，非 CTSS 本身。
2. 论文自陈其思想成于 MIT 两个委员会（Teager 长期计算需求委员会、McCarthy 计算机工作委员会），为“机构规划→工程实现”的链条提供一手节点，可用于 3.3.4 的延续与分流叙述。
3. 文中设施细节（Computation Center 的 7090、19 台磁带机、Direct Data Connection）是 3.3.1 资助地位的物质侧证据。
