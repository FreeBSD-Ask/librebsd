## 逐字摘录（正文）

> To meet the requirements of the Morrill Act, the State Legislature in Monterey passed the Organic Act of 1868, principally authored by Assemblymember John Dwinelle and signed by Governor Henry H. Haight. The Organic Act officially established the University of California as the state's land-grant university and established the Board of Regents and the Academic Senate. (Douglass 2020)

> The Organic Act also set the precedent of free tuition to all residents of California. Though students have been assessed registration fees, student fees, and other "incidental" fees since the university's inception, tuition was not officially imposed until 1970. (Vega 2014)

> The University of California was founded on March 23,1868, in , on the site of an existing private school called the College of California. This school, founded by Henry Durant and chartered in 1855, had been the state's first institution of higher learning. But due to insurmountable debts, the college later disincorporated and its buildings were deeded to the new University of California. Durant became UC's first president, serving two years. (OAC, Ohlone)

> On September 23, 1869, the University of California opened with six colleges:
> * College of Agriculture
> * College of Mechanic Arts
> * College of Letters
> * College of Chemistry
> * College of Mining
> * College of Civil Engineering

> In 1872, UC sold its Oakland buildings and established its permanent home in Berkeley.
