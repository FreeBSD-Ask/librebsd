## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

引用要素的层级：

> In general, citations to textual records of Federal agencies should identify the record item, the file unit, the series, the subgroup, the record group, and the repository.

要素不足的风险：

> Do not cite record group and box numbers only. One record group could have several boxes with the same number.

为何须引 subgroup：

> Cite the subgroup because identical series titles may be found throughout a record group. For questions concerning subgroup structure, consult the preliminary inventory or other finding aids.

引用的目的：

> As a rule, a citation to archival records should contain as much information as necessary to allow someone to easily locate those records.

## 三、考据说明

1. 六层要素（record item—file unit—series—subgroup—record group—repository）把“登记要素”从习惯做法变成可核对清单：缺哪一层，回溯就断在哪一层。
2. “Do not cite record group and box numbers only”直指常见错误：同一记录组内 box 号可重复，只写全宗与盒号等于没有定位。
3. subgroup 的必要性来自同名系列的存在——“identical series titles may be found throughout a record group”，故引文必须交代系列在机构内的位置，这也正是回溯核验的着手处。
4. 该册子同时说明“并非所有引用都具备全部要素”，即以“足以让他人定位”为充分标准——与项目“最小充分登记”的做法同向。
