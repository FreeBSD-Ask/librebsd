- **quote（逐字三处；原文行断按原样保留）**：
  1. “FreeBSD is an operating system based on 4.4 BSD Lite for Intel, AMD, Cyrix or NexGen "x86" based PC hardware.  It works with a very wide variety of PC peripherals and configurations and can be used for everything from software development to Internet Service Provision.”（What is FreeBSD? 段首四行）
  2. “Over 750 ports, from editors to programming languages to graphical applications, make FreeBSD a powerful and comprehensive operating environment that extends far beyond what's provided by many commercial versions of UNIX.”（ports 段四行）

- **考据注**：第 1 处是**独立发行实体定位**：基于 4.4 BSD Lite 的操作系统自述（x86 硬件域＋从软件开发到 ISP 的用途域）——独立 OS 身份由 2.x 发行层自述。第 2 处是**工程规模的计法侧面**：ports 逾 750（对照 2.0.5 发行说明的逾 270，ref11086 同构段）——两年间应用供给近三倍增长，工程量的人时规模由两发行层清单对照呈现；句尾“extends far beyond what's provided by many commercial versions of UNIX”是对商业 UNIX 的发行面超越表述。第 3 处是**发行节奏常态化**：与 2.0.5 说明同构的“一切齐备＋全源码在册＋一键整编”自述在 2.2.2 层再现——法律负担去除后的节奏常态由两发行层同构自证（2.0.5 版本措辞为 plus full source code，本处为 including，词差以两 ref 分立不撞四键）。**年代披露**：随 2.2.2-RELEASE（1997）发行。同文件 ABOUT.TXT 之 README.TXT（2.2.2）因 ASCII art 与正文混排不可作逐字引文，弃用。
