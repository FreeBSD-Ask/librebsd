## 正文摘录（逐字）

"The Project MAC contract between MIT and ARPA was signed on July 1, 1963."

"The Project MAC Computer Systems Research Group, led by MIT Prof. Corbató, accounted for about a third of Project MAC's budget; its goal was to design and implement a successor operating system to CTSS."

"Bell Telephone Laboratories became associated with the project in November 1964, at the suggestion of J. C. R. Licklider."

"Bell Laboratories withdrew from the Multics project in April 1969."

"During this time period, Bell Labs and GE/Honeywell also contributed resources to the development of Multics."

## 提要

multicians.org 记：MIT 与 ARPA 的 Project MAC 合同于 1963 年 7 月 1 日签署；由 Corbató 教授领导的计算机系统研究组约占 Project MAC 预算的三分之一，目标是设计并实现 CTSS 的后续操作系统；贝尔电话实验室经利克莱德建议于 1964 年 11 月加入该项目，并于 1969 年 4 月退出；在此期间贝尔实验室与 GE／霍尼韦尔也向 Multics 研制投入资源。
