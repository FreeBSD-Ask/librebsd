## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译；原句内直引号照录）

起源句：

> FOSDEM began in 2000 when Raphael Bauduin, a supporter of the Linux movement in Belgium, organised a small gathering for Open Source developers called the "Open Source Developers' European Meeting" (OSDEM).

后续句（创始与会者）：

> A mailing list, a simple website, and word of mouth quickly attracted interest, and several prominent figures in the Open Source community, including Carsten Haitzler (Rasterman), Gordon Lyon (Fyodor), and Jeremy Allison, joined the inaugural event.

更名句（同页，含全称展开）：

> The following year, OSDEM was renamed FOSDEM (Free and Open Source Software Developers' European Meeting).

今日规模句：

> Today, it hosts over 8000 developers annually at the ULB Solbosch campus in Brussels, offering a platform for collaboration across hundreds of projects and diverse technical tracks.

## 三、核对记录

- 官方口径：2000 年 Raphael Bauduin 组织 OSDEM（小型开发者聚会）；“The following year”更名 FOSDEM——与题面“2000 年以 OSDEM 起步，次年更名”吻合。
- 官方页未载更名绝对年份；首个以 FOSDEM 之名的实体届次通行记 2002 年 2 月（此为通行纪年，本页未载，不外推入 claim）。另一层纪年差异（首实办 2001-02 通行说）系“组织年／首办年”两口径之别，卡内披露。
- 本页更名句与 2025 届 About 页（ref O）互证（“For the second year, OSDEM was renamed FOSDEM.”）。
- 创会与会者（Haitzler／Lyon／Allison）与今日规模句入卡备用，本批 claim 未取。
