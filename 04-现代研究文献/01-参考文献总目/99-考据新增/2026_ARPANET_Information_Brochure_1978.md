## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

平台定位（不竞争商业服务）：

> The ARPANET is an operational DoD network and is not intended to compete with comparable commercial service.

用途限定：

> The ARPANET is intended to be used solely for the conduct of or in support of official U.S. Government business.

赞助者定义：

> A DoD or U.S. Government Agency, which qualifies as an ARPANET user, and is authorized to sponsor a contractor or other non-government activity for access to the ARPANET for the conduct of official U.S. Government business.

DCA 职责（节选）：

> (2) Approval of new user access.

赞助者名单（目录项）：

> Enclosure 6 List of ARPANET Sponsors

范围界定：

> ARPANET is an operational, resource sharing inter-computer network linking a wide variety of computers in CONUS, Hawaii, Norway, and England.

管理权移交：

> In July 1975, the DCA became the operational manager of the ARPANET.
