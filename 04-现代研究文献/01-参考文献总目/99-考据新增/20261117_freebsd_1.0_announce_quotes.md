## 逐字引文

Q1（1.4.2 旁证，首句与 11 月时点）:
The first "official" release of FreeBSD 1.0 is now available, no more greek letters - this is the "production" release.

Q2（1.4.1b，网络发行渠道）:
FreeBSD 1.0 RELEASE may be obtained by ftp on freebsd.cdrom.com, and on its various mirror sites within a day or so.

Q3（旁证，公告头日期行）:
Date: 1 Nov 1993 16:12:20 -0800

Q4（b007 1.6.4 B 源，公告头新闻组／主题／日期连续段）:
Newsgroups: comp.os.386bsd.announce Subject: FreeBSD 1.0 RELEASE now available Date: 1 Nov 1993 16:12:20 -0800

Q5（b008 1.7.1 A 源，PC 消费级外设连续段）:
The Linux sound driver for Gravis UltraSound, SoundBlaster, etc. cards. Mitsumi CDROM interface and drive.

Q6（b008 1.7.2 A 源，ISA 总线设备探测句）:
More fail-safe probing of devices on the ISA bus. This makes it much harder for devices to conflict with each other.

Q7（b009 1.8.1B 源，自有渠道与团队署名收束段）:
Questions, as usual, should be sent to " freebsd-questions@freefall.cdrom.com ". Bug reports to " freebsd-bugs@freefall.cdrom.com " and general technical commentary to " freebsd-hackers@freefall.cdrom.com ". Regards, The FreeBSD Team.
