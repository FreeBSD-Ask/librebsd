## 正文摘录（逐字）

"In early 1967, the sales of the PDP-8/S were so strong that DEC was moved to break new ground in the computer marketplace by offering the PDP-8/S for retail sale on a cash-and-carry basis, available at every DEC field office in the United States."

"Until then, they concentrated on enlarging the PDP-8 market by introducing ever less expensive models. The effect of this strategy was the rapid opening of an immense market for small computers."

## 提要

琼斯记：1967 年初 PDP-8/S 销路极好，DEC 遂在计算机市场首创零售现购自运（cash-and-carry）方式，全美各 DEC 现场办事处均可提机——计算机首次这样卖。此前 DEC 以不断推出更便宜型号来扩大 PDP-8 市场，效果是迅速打开巨大的小型机市场。两条分别承 3.3.3 的直销与产品线分段两面。
