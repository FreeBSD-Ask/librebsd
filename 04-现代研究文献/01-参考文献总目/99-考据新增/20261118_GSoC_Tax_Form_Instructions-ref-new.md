## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

填报义务的适用范围：

> *Some* contributors will be required to fill out a tax form when signing up with Payoneer.

美国居民与在美工作者的义务：

> All U.S. residents (or resident aliens) or any contributor coding in the U.S. for any length of time during the GSoC program will need to complete a tax form.

多国纳税的可能：

> Depending on your tax residency and location(s) where you perform work, you may be required to pay taxes in more than one country.

主办方不提供税务意见：

> Google can not provide you with tax advice.

## 三、读用界限

1. 页面只规定“须填报税表”，不载具体扣缴率；不得据此推断各国实收比例。
2. 1099-NEC 仅发给完成 W-9 的美国居民或居留外国人，与“所有参与者”不同，引用时不可混同。
3. 税表经 Payoneer 向导选择，主办方与 Payoneer 均不代为判断表种。
