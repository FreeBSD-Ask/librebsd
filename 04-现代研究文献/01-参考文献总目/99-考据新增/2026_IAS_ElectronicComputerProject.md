## 正文摘录（逐字）

"In late 1945, the Institute for Advanced Study embarked on a project that departed from the realm of the purely theoretical."

"With no laboratory facilities, the Institute was not, at first sight, an obvious choice for Professor John von Neumann's Electronic Computer Project."

"In the spring of 1945, von Neumann drafted a document that described the logical structure of a desired high-speed automatic digital computing system powerful enough to solve complex mathematical problems, such as non linear partial differential equations (of two or three independent variables), in abstract terms drawn from biology."

"Von Neumann's logical schema served as the basis for subsequent stored-program computers."

"In identifying the organs required as those relating to arithmetic, memory, control, and input and output devices, subsequently known as von Neumann Architecture, he laid down the basic schema of the modern computer."

"Differences over individual contributions and patents divided the group at the Moore School."

"In keeping with the spirit of academic enquiry, von Neumann was determined that advances be kept in the public domain. ECP progress reports were widely disseminated. As a consequence, the project had widespread influence."

"Copies of the IAS machine appeared nationally: AVIDAC at Argonne National Laboratory; ILLIAC at the University of Illinois; JOHNNIAC at RAND Corporation; MANIAC at Los Alamos Scientific Laboratory; ORACLE at Oak Ridge National Laboratory; ORDVAC at Aberdeen Proving Grounds; and internationally: BESK in Stockholm; BESM in Moscow; DASK in Denmark; PERM in Munich; SILLIAC in Sydney; and WEIZAC in Rehovoth, to mention a few."

"The IAS machine was used continually and productively until 1960, when it was given to the Smithsonian National Museum of American History's Computer History Collection."

## 提要

IAS 官方项目史记：1945 年底，普林斯顿高等研究院启动电子计算机项目（ECP）；研究院本无实验室设施，起初并不像是合适选址，项目最初只能栖身 Fuld Hall 地下室。1945 年春，冯·诺伊曼起草了一份文件，以取自生物学的抽象术语描述一台高速自动数字计算系统的逻辑结构，用以求解非线性偏微分方程等复杂问题；其逻辑图式成为后续存储程序计算机的基础，把所需器官分为算术、存储、控制与输入输出四类，后世称“冯·诺伊曼架构”。该页并记：Moore School 内部因个人贡献与专利问题产生分歧；冯·诺伊曼主张成果留在公有领域，ECP 进展报告广泛散发，项目因此影响广远。IAS 机的复制机在美国国内有 Argonne 的 AVIDAC、伊利诺伊大学的 ILLIAC、RAND 的 JOHNNIAC、洛斯阿拉莫斯的 MANIAC、橡树岭的 ORACLE、阿伯丁试验场的 ORDVAC，国外有斯德哥尔摩 BESK、莫斯科 BESM、丹麦 DASK、慕尼黑 PERM、悉尼 SILLIAC、雷霍沃特 WEIZAC 等。IAS 机一直使用至 1960 年，后移交史密森尼美国国家历史博物馆计算机史收藏。
