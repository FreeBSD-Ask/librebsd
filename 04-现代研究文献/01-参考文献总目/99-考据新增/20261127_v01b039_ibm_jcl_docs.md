## 正文摘录（逐字）

"Some z/OS users use the older term JCL card instead of JCL statement, because JCL used to be submitted to the system in the form of punched cards."

"All jobs require the three main types of JCL statements: JOB, EXEC, and DD."

"Because JCL was originally designed for punched cards, the details of coding JCL statements can be complicated."

## 提要

IBM z/OS 基础文档记：部分用户仍用旧称“JCL card”而非“JCL statement”，因为 JCL 过去以打孔卡形式提交；所有作业都需要 JOB、EXEC、DD 三类主要语句；JCL 原为打孔卡设计，故语句编码细节可很复杂。三条把作业控制语言的卡片起源与语句结构写明，与 1973 年同期手册互证。
