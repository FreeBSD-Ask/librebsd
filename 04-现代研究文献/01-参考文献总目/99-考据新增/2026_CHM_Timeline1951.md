## 正文摘录（逐字）

"First Univac 1 delivered to US Census Bureau"

"The Univac 1 is the first commercial computer to attract widespread public attention."

"Although manufactured by Remington Rand, the machine was often mistakenly referred to as 'the IBM Univac.'"

"Univac computers were used in many different applications but utilities, insurance companies and the US military were major customers."

"Created by Presper Eckert and John Mauchly -- designers of the earlier ENIAC computer -- the Univac 1 used 5,200 vacuum tubes and weighed 29,000 pounds."

"Remington Rand eventually sold 46 Univac 1s at more than $1 million each."

"The title of 'first commercially available general-purpose computer' probably goes to Britain's Ferranti Mark I for its sale of its first Mark I computer to Manchester University."

"Modeled after the Cambridge University EDSAC computer, the president of Lyons Tea Co. has the LEO built to solve the problem of production scheduling and delivery of cakes to the hundreds of Lyons tea shops around England."

"The LEO was England's first commercial computer and was performing useful work before any other commercial computer system in the world."

## 提要

Computer History Museum 1951 年时间线记：首台 UNIVAC 1 交付美国人口普查局；UNIVAC 1 是第一台引起公众广泛注意的商用计算机，虽由 Remington Rand 制造，却常被误称为“IBM Univac”；其用户遍及多行业，公用事业、保险公司与美军为主要客户；机器由 ENIAC 设计者 Eckert 与 Mauchly 创制，用 5,200 只真空管、重 29,000 磅，Remington Rand 最终以每台逾百万美元售出 46 台。同一页并记：“第一台商用通用计算机”之名大概应归英国 Ferranti Mark I，因其首台售予曼彻斯特大学；里昂茶业公司总裁以剑桥 EDSAC 为蓝本建造 LEO，用于解决向英格兰数百家茶馆配送蛋糕的生产排程问题，LEO 是英格兰第一台商用计算机，且在世界任何商用计算机系统之前已在做有用工作。

## 存疑与边界

- 馆方对“第一台商用通用计算机”用“probably”限定，并归 Ferranti Mark I；与 Britannica 称 UNIVAC 为“最早商用计算机之一”并置时须保留各自限定，块内不合并为单一断言。
- 页面未给 Census Bureau 交付的具体月日；Britannica 记为 1951 年 3 月。
