  1. This document provides a minimum set of guidelines for Network Etiquette (Netiquette) which organizations may take and adapt for their own use. As such, it is deliberately written in a bulleted format to make adaptation easier and to make any particular item easy (or easier) to find.（Abstract 段）
  2. You should not send heated messages … even if you are provoked. On the other hand, you shouldn't be surprised if you get flamed and it's prudent not to respond to flames. … Don't get involved in flame wars. Neither post nor respond to incendiary material.（2.1 邮件区与 2.2 新闻组区各一段；省略号跳过 ("flames") 直引号短语与另条 bullet，引擎逐段校验）
