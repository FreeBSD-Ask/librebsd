## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

CSNET 自 1985 年起自给，并与 BITNET 合并为 CREN：

> CSNET was established in 1981 with initial grant funding from the National Science Foundation, and has been self-supporting since 1985.

> CSNET is now merged with BITNET to form the Corporation for Research and Education Networking (CREN).

BITNET 由 CREN 运营、EDUCOM 设网络信息中心：

> BITNET is operated by CREN with a Network Information Center operated by EDUCOM.

## 三、考据说明
