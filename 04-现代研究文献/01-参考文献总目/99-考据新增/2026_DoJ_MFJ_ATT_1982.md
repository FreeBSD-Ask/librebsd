## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

> it is hereby ORDERED, ADJUDGED, AND DECREED that the Final Judgment entered on January 24, 1956, is hereby vacated in its entirety and replaced by the following items 'and provisions:

> 3. The termination of the License Contracts between AT&T and the SOCs and other subsidiaries and the Standard Supply Contract between Western Electric and the BOCs and other subsidiaries; and

> 4. The transfer of ownership of the separated portions of the BOCs providing local exchange and exchange access services from AT&T by means of a spin-off of stock of the separated BOCs to the shareholders of AT&T, or by other disposition

> 2. manufacture or provide telecommunications products or customer premises equipment (except for provision of customer premises equipment for emergency services); or

## 三、说明

令文废止 1956 年判决并规定重组四步：向 BOC 移交可独立运营交换与交换接入的设施、人员、系统与技术信息权；在 BOC 内部分离交换业务与其他业务（含长途交换传输与用户 premises 设备）的设施、人员与账簿；终止 AT&T 与子公司的许可合同及西电与 BOC 的标准供货合同；以向股东分拆股票方式转移分离部分所有权。第二编并禁止 BOC 自行制造通信产品与用户 premises 设备。制造主体（西电）本身留在 AT&T 内，被剥离的是本地交换公司与供货合同关系——本批据此在批记中标注题面“制造主体剥离”的辨析。
