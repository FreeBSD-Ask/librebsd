- 逐字引文（三段，以省略号连接；PDF 文本层有 OCR 噪声，所引三段各经比对无噪声）：

Systems Engineering Services- Effective June 23, 1969, IBM assistance in the installation and use of IBM data processing products will be provided on a charge basis under an Agreement for IBM Systems Engineering Services. … Program Products are related to the application of a system to user tasks … and will be offered on an individual charge basis under a License Agreement for IBM Program Products.

- 卡内说明：
  - 前段取自公告信正文（Systems Engineering Services 条），后两段取自 Programs 条的 Program Products 句；中段省略号跳过 “（such as language compilers, general purpose utility programs, and industry .de a.ther application programs)”（该括注含 OCR 噪声 “.de a.ther”）。
  - 信件落款 W. G. Rodgers（Data Processing Division），信头日期 OCR 为 “~une 23, 1969”；正文三处 “Effective June 23, 1969” 逐字可读，日期以正文为准。
  - 硬事实（第四份核查）：1969-06-23 公告日由本信正文三处自证，并与 ADAPSO 口述史（“the announcement was made on June 23, 1969”）互证；收费制 1970-01-01 起全面生效由信内豁免条款（“prior to January 1, 1970 will be waived”）与口述史（“as of January 1, 1970, you'd have to pay”）互证。
  - 用途：V02.1.4.6（系统软件捆绑的反垄断争议）第二源；软件独立定价与许可实践的当事人一手文书。
