- **数据库**：新建 ref（2026-11-16 V35 P2 联网补证批 b013；查重：库内 RFC 1122 为 ref10828，`1123` 无条目——`source_url` 含 “1123” 的检索只命中无关 Apple 支持页）

- **quote（逐字，两处，分落两块）**：
  1. This RFC enumerates standard protocols that a host connected to the Internet must use, and it incorporates by reference the RFCs and other documents describing the current specifications for these protocols. It corrects errors in the referenced documents and adds additional discussion and guidance for an implementor.
  2. The following also made significant contributions to particular areas: Eric Allman (Berkeley), Rob Austein (MIT), Art Berggreen (ACC), Keith Bostic (Berkeley), Vint Cerf (NRI), Wayne Hathaway (NASA), Matt Korn (IBM), Erik Naggum (Naggum Software, Norway), Robert Ullmann (Prime Computer), David Waitzman (BBN), Frank Wancho (USA), Arun Welch (Ohio State), Bill Westfield (Cisco), and Rayan Zachariassen (Toronto).
- **计数口径（要紧）**：第 2 处所配套的 fact 断言“**全文不出现 BSD 字样**”，此判据按抓取文本实测 `BSD` 出现 0 次、`Berkeley` 3 次（皆机构归属用法，见 §1.4 致谢两段）；**计数只对应当前抓取版本**。跨文本对照：姊妹篇 RFC 1122（ref10828）全文 `4.2BSD` 1 次、`4.3BSD` 1 次，出现在一处脚注（“*4.2BSD Unix and its derivatives, but not 4.3BSD”，该句已由 CL-RM-495906 用于 V31 7.6.5，本批不重复建 claim）。两篇并置即为“出处注记分布稀疏且不均”的实证。
