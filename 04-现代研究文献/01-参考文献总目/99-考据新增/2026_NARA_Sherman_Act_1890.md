## 二、逐字引文（英文转录，未翻译）

立法经过（页面表述）：

> On April 8, 1890, the Senate passed the bill by a 51-to-1 vote; the House of Representatives passed it on June 20 by a unanimous vote of 242 to 0, and President Benjamin Harrison signed the bill into law on July 2, 1890.

第一节第一句（1890 年原文）：

> Every contract, combination in the form of trust or otherwise, or conspiracy, in restraint of trade or commerce among the several States, or with foreign nations, is hereby declared to be illegal.

第一节罚则（1890 年原文）：

> Every person who shall make any such contract or engage in any such combination or conspiracy, shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor, and, on conviction thereof, shall be punished by fine not exceeding five thousand dollars, or by imprisonment not exceeding one year, or by both said punishments, at the discretion of the court.

第七节三倍赔偿（1890 年原文）：

> Any person who shall be injured in his business or property by any other person or corporation by reason of anything forbidden or declared to be unlawful by this act, may sue therefor in any circuit court of the United States in the district in which the defendant resides or is found, without respect to the amount in controversy, and shall recover three fold the damages by him sustained, and the costs of suit, including a reasonable attorney's fee.

## 三、考据说明

1. 本页为联邦档案机构对原件（enrolled act）的正式转录，属官方一手；表决数字（51–1、242–0）与签署日期（1890-07-02）可与国会记录交叉。
2. **1890 原文与现行法典的两处实差异**（对照 LII 编码本）：①首句 1890 作 "is **hereby** declared to be illegal"，现行版无 "hereby"；②罚则 1890 作轻罪（misdemeanor）与 5,000 美元／一年监禁，现行版为重罪（felony）与 1 亿美元（公司）／100 万美元（个人）／10 年，经 1937、1955、1974、1990、2004 年多次上调。第七节三倍赔偿自 1890 年延续至今。
