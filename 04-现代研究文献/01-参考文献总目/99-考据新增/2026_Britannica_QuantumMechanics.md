## 正文摘录（逐字）

"Then, in the 12 months started in July 1925, a period of creativity without parallel in the history of physics, there appeared a series of papers by German scientists that set the subject on a firm conceptual foundation."

"The papers took two approaches: (1) matrix mechanics, proposed by Werner Heisenberg, Max Born, and Pascual Jordan, and (2) wave mechanics, put forward by Erwin Schrödinger."

"However, Schrödinger, not allowing his emotions to interfere with his scientific endeavours, showed that, in spite of apparent dissimilarities, the two theories are equivalent mathematically."

"In spite of the overwhelming practical success of quantum mechanics, the foundations of the subject contain unresolved problems—in particular, problems concerning the nature of measurement."

## 提要

Britannica 记：1925 年 7 月起的十二个月是物理学史上无可比拟的创造期，一批德国科学家的论文把量子力学置于牢固的概念基础之上；论文取两条路径——海森堡、玻恩与约当的矩阵力学，以及薛定谔的波动力学；薛定谔随后证明两套理论在数学上等价。该条同时指出，尽管量子力学在实践上极为成功，其基础仍含未决问题，尤其是测量问题。

## 存疑与边界
