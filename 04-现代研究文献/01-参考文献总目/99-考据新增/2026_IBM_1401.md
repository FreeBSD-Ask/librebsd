## 正文摘录（逐字）

"Introduced in 1959, the IBM 1401 quickly made inroads into the business community."

"It was one of the first computers to run on transistors instead of power-hungry and fragile vacuum tubes, making it far more reliable and compact than existing mainframes."

"By the mid-1960s, it comprised more than half of the world's computers."

"By the mid-1960s, more than 10,000 of the 1401 systems were installed, making it by far the best-selling computer up to that point."

"The 1401 changed perceptions among business executives. A computer didn't have to be as big as a room and at the service of just large corporations — it could fit comfortably in the office of a medium-size company or lab, and each department could afford to have one of its own."

## 提要

IBM 官方企业史记：IBM 1401 于 1959 年推出，迅速进入商业界；它是最早以晶体管取代耗电而脆弱的真空管的计算机之一，因而比当时的大型机更可靠、更紧凑；至 1960 年代中期，1401 占全世界计算机的一半以上，装机逾一万台，是此前最畅销的计算机。该页并称 1401 改变了企业主管的观念：计算机不必占满一室、只服务大公司，中型企业或实验室的办公室即可容纳，每个部门都买得起。
