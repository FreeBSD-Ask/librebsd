# Debian 官方移植索引（debian.org/ports，2016 年 Wayback 存档）引用卡

- **性质**：Debian 官网 Ports 索引页，2016-01-07 Wayback 快照（web.archive.org/web/20160107055849），页面分“List of official ports”与“List of unofficial ports”两清单
- **取件**：Tavily extract 2026-11-28，快照 `script/_v57_wave1/7.1/snapshots/debian_ports_index_wb.txt`（11 807 B，UTF-8）
- **四内核配对句（V57.7.1.2 B 块）**：while most Debian ports are based on Linux, there also are ports based on the FreeBSD, NetBSD and Hurd kernels.
- **kFreeBSD 编目行（V57.7.1.5 E 块前半）**：First officially released with Debian 6.0 as a technology preview and the first non-Linux port released by Debian. Port of the Debian GNU system to the kernel of FreeBSD.
- **netbsd-i386 编目行（V57.7.1.1 A 块；V57.7.1.5 E 块后半）**：A port of the Debian operating system, complete with apt, dpkg, and GNU userland, to the NetBSD kernel. The port, never released, has been abandoned.
- **编目位置**：kfreebsd-amd64／kfreebsd-i386 两行列于“List of official ports”清单（状态 released）；netbsd-i386／netbsd-alpha 两行列于“List of unofficial ports”清单（状态 dead）。
