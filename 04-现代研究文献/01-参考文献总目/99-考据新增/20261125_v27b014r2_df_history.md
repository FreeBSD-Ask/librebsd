## 逐字引文

Q1（4.2.5A 源，集群目标弱化与文件系统一致性保留）:
The ultimate goal of the DragonFly project at its inception was to provide native clustering support in the kernel. … This original design direction, although no longer the primary goal of the DragonFly project, has influenced many of the design decisions made in the intervening years. While full cache coherency is no longer a top level goal, filesystem coherency is, and that direction continues to guide the project in a number of ways.

Q2（4.3.2B 源，回顾“四版延续”定位及其过时）:
DragonFly BSD was forked from FreeBSD 4.8 in June of 2003, by Matthew Dillon. The project was originally billed as "the logical continuation of the FreeBSD 4.x series", as quoted in the announcement, but this description has long since become obsolete.
