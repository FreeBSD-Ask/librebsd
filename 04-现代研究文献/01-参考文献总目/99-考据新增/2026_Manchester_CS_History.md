## 正文摘录（逐字）

"The world's first stored-program electronic digital computer - the Small-Scale Experimental Machine, known as SSEM, or the 'Baby' - was designed and built by F.C. Williams and Tom Kilburn at The University of Manchester, and made its first successful run of a program on 21 June 1948."

"The Baby was the first machine that had all the components of a modern computer."

"Most importantly, it was the first computer that could store not only data but any short user program in electronic memory, and process it at electronic speed."

"Alan Turing was the Deputy Director of the University's Computing Machines Laboratory from 1948 to 1954 where he proposed the Turing Test and worked on biological morphogenesis among many other innovative concepts in computer science."

"Following this early pioneering work the Department of Computer Science was founded in 1964, when Tom Kilburn became the ﬁrst Professor of Computer Science and Head of Department."

## 提要

曼彻斯特大学计算机科学系系史记：世界第一台存储程序电子数字计算机——小型实验机 SSEM，通称“Baby”——由 F. C. Williams 与 Tom Kilburn 在该校设计建造，1948 年 6 月 21 日首次成功运行程序；Baby 是第一台具备现代计算机全部部件的机器，最重要的是第一台能把数据与任意短用户程序都存入电子存储器、并以电子速度处理的计算机。图灵 1948—1954 年任该校计算机器实验室副主任。1964 年计算机科学系成立，Kilburn 出任首任教授兼系主任。

## 存疑与边界

- 系史页用“world's first stored-program electronic digital computer”的绝对表述；本批与 Nature 1948 原文（未用“first”字样，只述实验机与存储原理）并置，块内保留各源原表述。
- 页面未给 Baby 的存储容量与程序内容。
