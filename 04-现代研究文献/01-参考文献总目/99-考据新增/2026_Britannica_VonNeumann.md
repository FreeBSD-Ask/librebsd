## 正文摘录（逐字）

"Von Neumann pioneered game theory and, along with Alan Turing and Claude Shannon, was one of the conceptual inventors of the stored-program digital computer."

"Von Neumann's gift for applied mathematics took his work in directions that influenced quantum theory, automata theory, economics, and defense planning."

## 提要

Britannica 传记记：冯·诺伊曼开创博弈论，并与图灵、香农同为存储程序数字计算机的概念发明者之一；其应用数学的才能把工作引向量子理论、自动机理论、经济学与国防规划等方向。
