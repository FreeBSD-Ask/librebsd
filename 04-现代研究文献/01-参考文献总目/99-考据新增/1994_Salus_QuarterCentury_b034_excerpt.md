## 正文摘录（逐字）

### 块 150（CTSS 硬件、用户规模、服务与实验室双重身份）

> The Compatible Timesharing System (CTSS) was one of the first timesharing systems. Developed at the MIT Computation Center by a team led by Corbato, CTSS ran on a modified IBM 7094 with a second 32K-word bank of memory, using two IBM 7320As. A second 7094 was connected in early 1965.

> Remote access was provided to up to 30 users via an IBM 7750 communications controller connected to dial-up modems for access over phone lines.

> CTSS, as the timesharing portion of Project MAC, was both a service facility and a laboratory for system programmers.

### 块 146（Project MAC 建制、1963 夏研访问者）

> Project MAC was organized at MIT in the spring of 1963 at the suggestion of J. C. R. Licklider. Its founding director was MIT Professor Robert M. Fano.

> In 1963 Project MAC hosted a summer study, which brought many well-known computer scientists to Cambridge to use CTSS (the Compatible Timesharing System) on the IBM and to discuss the future of computing.

> At this time, the major efforts of Project MAC were Fernando J. Corbato's CTSS, Victor H. Yngve's COMIT, and Minsky's AI Lab.

### 块 166（CTSS/Multics 经验向 Ritchie 与 Thompson 的传递）

> The most important effect may well have been on Dennis Ritchie and Ken Thompson of Bell Labs, and their colleagues—Mcllroy, Morris, Neumann, and Ossanna—who had been exposed to CTSS and to Multics.

## 提要

Salus 1994 年权威叙述：CTSS 由 Corbató 团队在 MIT 计算中心开发，运行于改装 IBM 7094（增第二组 32K 字内存、两台 7320A），1965 年初接入第二台 7094；经 IBM 7750 通信控制器与拨号调制解调器提供至多 30 用户的远程访问；CTSS 作为 Project MAC 的分时部分，兼具服务设施与系统程序员实验室两种身份。Project MAC 1963 年由利克莱德倡议、Fano 任首任主任组建，1963 年办夏季研究，吸引众多知名计算机科学家到剑桥使用 CTSS。Corbató 的 CTSS 经验经人员接触传给贝尔实验室的 Ritchie、Thompson 等人。均为权威二手叙述，作背景性材料，须与一手交叉验证。
