## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

> The Commitment set a national policy favoring regulated monopoly over competition but failed to consider the steps that government could have taken to boost competition, such as the establishment of interconnection obligations between competing local telephone companies.

> This was the Era of Regulation: when monopoly was considered an act of nature and government stood in the shoes of consumers.

## 三、说明

FCC 总法律顾问的官方文稿把金斯伯里承诺定位为“确立以受管制垄断取代竞争的国家政策”，并指其未考虑互连义务等促竞争措施；同时以“监管时代”概括 1913 年至 1980 年代初的政策取向。与 Mueller 书章互证：一为监管机构自述，一为学术批判判读。
