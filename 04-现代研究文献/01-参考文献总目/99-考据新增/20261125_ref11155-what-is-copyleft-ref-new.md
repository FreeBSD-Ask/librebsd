- **quote（逐字，取用于本批）**：
  1. That's why we reverse the name, changing “copyright” into “copyleft.”
  2. Copyleft says that anyone who redistributes the software, with or without changes, must pass along the freedom to further copy and change it. Copyleft guarantees that every user has freedom.
  3. Copyleft is a general concept, and you can't use a general concept directly; you can only use a specific implementation of the concept.
