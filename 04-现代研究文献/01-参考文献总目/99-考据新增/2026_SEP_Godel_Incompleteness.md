## 正文摘录（逐字）

"Gödel’s two incompleteness theorems are among the most important results in modern logic, and have deep implications for various issues. They concern the limits of provability in formal axiomatic theories."

"The first incompleteness theorem states that in any consistent formal system \(F\) within which a certain amount of arithmetic can be carried out, there are statements of the language of \(F\) which can neither be proved nor disproved in \(F\)."

"According to the second incompleteness theorem, such a formal system cannot prove that the system itself is consistent (assuming it is indeed consistent)."

"In the case of the second theorem, \(F\) must contain a little bit more arithmetic than in the case of the first theorem, which holds under very weak conditions."

"It is important to note that this result, like the first incompleteness theorem, is a theorem about formal provability, or derivability (which is always relative to some formal system)."

## 提要

SEP 记：哥德尔两条不完全性定理关涉形式公理理论中可证性的界限；第一定理称，任何可承载一定算术之一致形式系统内存在既不可证亦不可否证之语句；第二定理称此等系统不能自证其一致性；第二定理所需算术略强于第一定理；两定理均为关于形式可证性（相对某形式系统之可导性）的结论。
