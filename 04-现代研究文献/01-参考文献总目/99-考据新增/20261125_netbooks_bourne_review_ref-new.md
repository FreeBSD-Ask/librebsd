## 逐字摘录（正文）

> The remaining chapters are each dedicated to a single subject: editing, the shell, C, writing programs, document preparation, and data manipulation tool such as awk, sed, sort and their friends. There are also several appendices containing many manual pages from the UPM; summaries of adb, ed, sh, troff, and vi; a sample troff macro library; a summary of the -ms macro library; and the ASCII character set.

> The book is well written and very readable. There are numerous examples illustrating the text. He does not use the "sample dialogue" form of example that many authors seem to prefer, but usually shows a sample command and describes the results. This may be harder for some readers to follow, but is the only way to describe interactions with programs such as the vi editor.

> A motivated reader should be able to become a knowledgeable user of the Unix system by reading and understanding this book. Little supplementary material should be needed, since the appendices contain most of the Unix programmers manual.
