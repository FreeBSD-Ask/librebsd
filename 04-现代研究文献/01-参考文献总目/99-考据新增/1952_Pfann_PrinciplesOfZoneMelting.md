## 摘要摘录（逐字）

"In zone-melting, a small molten zone or zones traverse a long charge of alloy or impure metal."

"Consequences of this manner of freezing are examined with respect to solute distribution in the ingot, with particular reference to purification and to prevention of segregation."

"Results are expressed in terms of the number, size, and direction of travel of the zones, the initial solute distribution, and the distribution coefficient."

## 提要

Pfann 1952 年论文摘要称：区熔法中一个或多个狭窄熔区通过长锭料（合金或不纯金属）；论文考察这种凝固方式对锭内溶质分布的影响，尤其着眼于提纯与防止偏析，并以熔区数目、尺寸、移动方向、初始溶质分布与分配系数表述结果。作者单位为贝尔电话实验室 Murray Hill。
