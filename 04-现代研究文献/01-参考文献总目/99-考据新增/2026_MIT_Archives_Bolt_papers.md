机构起源（3.2.1）：

> In 1948 Bolt started a consulting firm with fellow MIT professor, Leo Beranek. Robert Newman, a former student of Bolt’s, joined the firm in 1950, forming Bolt Beranek Newman (BBN). Bolt remained as chairman in the firm until 1976. BBN developed ARPANET, a forerunner of the internet.

声学学术背景（3.2.1）：

> Richard H. Bolt, 1911-2002, was a professor of physics at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT). His main area of study was acoustics.
