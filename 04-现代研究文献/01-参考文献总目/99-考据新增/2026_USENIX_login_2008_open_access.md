## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

宣布日期与决定：

> On March 12, 2008, the USENIX Association Board and staff announced publicly that access to all of our conference proceedings would now be available free of charge to the general public.

决定的定位与意图：

> In making this move we hope to set the standard for open access to information, an essential part of our mission. This significant decision will allow universal access to some of the most important technical research in advanced computing.
