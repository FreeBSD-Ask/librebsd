## 正文摘录（逐字）

"System/360 is a single system spanning the performance range of virtually all current IBM computers - - from the widely used 1401 to nearly twice that of the most powerful computer previously built by the company."

"System/360 monthly rentals will range from $2,700 for a basic configuration to $115,000 for a typical large multisystem configuration. Comparable purchase prices range from $133,000 to $5,500,000."

"Incorporated with these are more than 40 types of peripheral equipment which store information and enter it into and retrieve it from the computer."

"The equipment is supported by programs which enable System/360 to schedule its own activities for non-stop computing that makes most efficient use of system capabilities."

"With the same type of input/output devices, a user can expand his System/360 to any point in its performance range, without reprogramming."

## 提要

IBM 数据处理部 1964-04-07 新闻稿称：System/360 是覆盖公司现有计算机性能跨度的单一系统，从广泛使用的 1401 到此前最强机器的近两倍；月租 2,700—115,000 美元、售价 133,000—5,500,000 美元并列公布；随处理器一并公布 40 余种外围设备，设备由程序支撑以实现不间断计算；用户可在同一输入输出设备下把系统扩展到性能区间任意点而无需重写程序。五条分别承 3.2.1 的租赁销售、3.2.3 的单系统覆盖、3.2.4 的外围与软件同步、3.2.6 的兼容标准。
