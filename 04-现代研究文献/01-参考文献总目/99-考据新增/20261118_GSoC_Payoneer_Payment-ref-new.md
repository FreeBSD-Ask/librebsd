## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

费用界分（汇兑一层由参与者承担）：

> Google covers a majority of the other fees around your cards or bank transfers, but does not cover currency exchange fees.

注册截止与首期时点：

> You must complete your Payoneer registration (including tax forms where appropriate) before June 20th to receive your first stipend on time.

账户审核时滞：

> After you register, your Payoneer account will be reviewed. It usually takes about a week before you receive an email that your account is approved.

注册失败的后果（改件即封号）：

> If you send a document that has been altered in ANY way you will very likely be banned from creating a Payoneer account, which means you can't receive any payment for participation in GSoC.

## 三、读用界限

1. “majority of the other fees”为限定表述，页面未列举中转行手续费名目；不得据以断言全部费用均由主办方承担。
2. 注册截止日与首期发放日为 2026 年度口径，历年日期不同，不得回推。
3. 页面自述“账户审核通常约一周”，属经验值，不构成承诺；延误个案仍须以当事人记录为据。
