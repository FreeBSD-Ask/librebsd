## 正文摘录（逐字）

"On 18 October 1954, in Dallas, Texas Instruments announced the first commercial transistor pocket radio, the Regency TR1, created in collaboration with IDEA Corporation and available in time for the holiday shopping season."

"By May 1954, TI surprised its rival licensees by making silicon transistors that worked."

"Haggerty pushed TI's engineers to develop transistors made of silicon, rather than germanium, because they could work at higher temperatures and therefore have more applications in the industrial and military markets."

"Miniaturization became the standard for the industry."

"Transistors changed the way people listened to music, watched television, and, by the 1970s, interacted with computers."

## 提要

ETHW 条目记：1954 年 10 月 18 日，德州仪器在达拉斯宣布首款商用晶体管袖珍收音机 Regency TR1（与 IDEA 公司合作，赶在假日购物季上市）；同年 5 月，TI 已制成可工作的硅晶体管，令同类被许可方意外；副总裁 Haggerty 推动以硅代锗，因硅管可在更高温度工作、在工业与军用市场用途更广。条目并称小型化自此成为行业标准，晶体管改变了人们听音乐、看电视的方式，并在 1970 年代改变人机交互。

## 存疑与边界

- 条目未载 TI 硅晶体管的具体型号与产量；TR-1 的商业成败另有“modest commercial success”表述，本批不引。
- “小型化成为行业标准”为条目对行业趋势的概括，非统计结论。
