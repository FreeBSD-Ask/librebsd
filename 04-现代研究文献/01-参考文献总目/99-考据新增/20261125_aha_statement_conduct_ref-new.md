## 关键原文（逐字）

> Certain kinds of research, certain forms of employment, and certain techniques (for instance, in conducting oral history interviews) sometimes entail promises about what a historian will and will not do with the resulting knowledge.

> They should define any confidentiality requirements before their research begins, and give public notice of any conditions or rules that may affect the content of their work.

> Scholarship flourishes in an atmosphere of openness and candor, which should include the scrutiny and public discussion of academic deception.

## 中文提要

AHA 声明在“Scholarship”节确立三类义务：特定研究与口述史访谈会产生对受访者的承诺（保密与使用限度）；研究者须在研究开始前界定保密要求并公开告知可能影响研究内容的规则；“Plagiarism”节末把开放坦诚界定为学术繁荣条件，明列对学术欺骗的审视与公开讨论。三句分别为 3.7.3（未获同意的材料处理／承诺）、3.7.2（开工前落档）与 3.7.5（争议公开披露）提供一手规范依据。
