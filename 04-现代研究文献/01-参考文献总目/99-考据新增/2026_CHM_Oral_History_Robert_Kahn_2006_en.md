## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译；右单引号 U+2019 照录）

人员去向与主任更替（2.1.2B，Kahn 述 Roberts 离职去向）：

> No, and it wasn’t a swapping of positions because Larry replaced Steve Levy running Telenet. They hired him in as the CEO

交付系统与设备归宿（2.1.4B，ARPANET 退役）：

> The ARPANET then ran with the TCP/IP protocols until it was decommissioned sometime in 1990. I think it was actually in 1990 that the last node was decommissioned.

收尾记录的不确定（2.1.6B，当事人不知记录所在）：

> One would have to go back to the record, and I don’t know exactly where that would be located. It may be in the archives.

## 三、考据说明

1. Kahn 为 IPTO 1979—1985 主任、TCP/IP 共同作者，其口述史覆盖 IPTO 人事链（Roberts 1973-09 离任去 Telenet→Licklider 1974 回任→Russell→Kahn）与 ARPANET 1990 年退役，是 2.1 节“人员去向”“设备归宿”两题的直接一手。
