人员去向与主任更替（2.1.2B，Kahn 述 Roberts 离职去向）：

> No, and it wasn’t a swapping of positions because Larry replaced Steve Levy running Telenet. They hired him in as the CEO

## 三、考据说明

1. Kahn 为 IPTO 1979—1985 主任、TCP/IP 共同作者，其口述史覆盖 IPTO 人事链（Roberts 1973-09 离任去 Telenet→Licklider 1974 回任→Russell→Kahn）与 ARPANET 1990 年退役，是 2.1 节“人员去向”“设备归宿”两题的直接一手。
2. 2.1.6 的引文（“不知道记录在哪里，也许在档案馆”）是“收尾留痕稀薄”的当事人自证，与 NRC 第 9 章章首的学术自述（“little information was drawn from the records…”）互为印证。

## 四、补充引文（b043 取用，2.2 节六题）

机构间流动路径（2.2.1A，Elkind 经 Licklider 入 MIT 再入 PARC）：

> Jerry ended up being hired by Licklider over to MIT for a while before he ended up going after Xerox PARC where, I guess, Bob Taylor hired him in as his boss.

招募方视角（2.2.5B，把聪明人拉进来互动）：

> I think they were just happy to bring smart people into the mix and have them interact with other people.

合作网络延续（2.2.2B，长期友谊）：

> I met many people there who I got to know and have maintained long friendships with ever since.

机构环境差异（2.2.4A，BBN 环境独特）：

> The environment there was unlike any other one I had ever been at.

个人去留抉择（2.2.3B）：

> Do I go back or do I stay with the ARPANET project? I opted to stay

记录存续依赖当事人（2.2.6B，Xerox 内情须问当事人）：

> I'm sure you could find people from the Xerox days that could explain it.

## 五、补充引文（b044 取用，2.3 节六题）

两用政策的操作定义（2.3.1B，SCP 首要把技术交国防部）：

> So goal number one was getting it to DOD. The strategy was you need industry help, because that’s what the DOD has to rely on.

立项建立在预算假定上（2.3.2B）：

> I was sort of hired to go do that on the assumption that it was in the budget and would happen, but it never actually happened.

保密与公开边界（2.3.4A，黑项目）：

> there were an increasing number of “black programs” that were being started. Things that I didn’t know about, couldn’t talk about if I did.

成文之外的经费政治（2.3.5B）：

> the politics of taking all the money and building it up in one office versus sharing a little bit of the wealth with the other offices politically probably didn’t work.

1980 年代中期后的财政节点（2.3.6B）：

> Gramm-Rudman was on the horizon and it eventually came back and bit DARPA pretty heavily.

## 六、补充引文（b045 取用，2.4 节三题）

资助决策与平台传导（2.4.1A，BBN Unix 包进 Berkeley 发行带）：

> The decision was made to have Berkeley put the BBN Unix software package on the Berkeley distribution tape.

传导环节中的人（2.4.4B，Duane Adams 拉 Berkeley 参与）：

> Duane Adams, who was working for me, had gotten the Berkeley guys involved in it.

解释限度（2.4.6B，当事人不知实现细节）：

> I don’t know how he did the actual implementation.

## 七、补充引文（b046 取用，2.5 节三题）

团队职级结构（2.5.1B，Frank Heart 与 Kahn 平行汇报）：

> He had another gentleman working for him named Frank Heart. So in some sense, Frank and I were parallel in terms of the reporting structure

设备选择（2.5.2A，Honeywell 机型由 Frank Heart 与 Hawley Rising 定）：

> The actual choice for the Honeywell machine was probably made by some combination of Frank Heart and Hawley Rising, who worked for them.

经费规模增长（2.5.4B，IPTO 预算随 VLSI 等扩张）：

> it took the IPTO budget and it grew it by leaps and bounds. It went from mainly the $40 million-ish level to maybe the $90 million level with the addition of VLSI and other growth that got built into the program.

## 八、补充引文（b048 取用，2.4 节两题）

采用链条的机制（2.4.3A，单包经商业厂商分发成为技术载体）：

> by getting it through a single package from a commercial vendor, you could go buy their workstation or their operating system and it came packaged for free. So it became the carrier of that part of the technology base.

扩散断言（2.4.5A，早期互联网基础设施经 SCP 落地）：

> A lot of the infrastructure for the early Internet happened through Strategic Computing, which caused it to get funded and in place.
