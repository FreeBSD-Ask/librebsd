## 摘要摘录（逐字，Crossref 官方元数据 abstract 字段）

"The application of quantum mechanics to the problem of metallic conduction has cleared up many of the difficulties which were so apparent in the free electron theories of Drude and Lorentz."

"Sommerfeld assumed that the valency electrons of the metallic atoms formed an electron gas which obeyed the Fermi-Dirac statistics, instead of Maxwellian statistics, and, using in the main classical ideas, showed how the difficulty of the specific heat would be removed."

"F. Bloch took up the question of the mechanics of electrons in a metallic lattice, and showed that if the lattice is perfect an electron can travel quite freely through it."

"Therefore so long as the lattice is perfect the conductivity is infinite, and it is only when we take into account the thermal motion and the impurities that we obtain a finite value for the conductivity."

## 提要

Wilson 1931 年论文摘要开篇即称：把量子力学应用于金属导电问题，澄清了德鲁德与洛伦兹自由电子理论中许多明显的困难。摘要接着梳理该路线：索末菲以服从费米—狄拉克统计的电子气取代麦克斯韦统计，解决了比热困难；布洛赫研究金属晶格中电子的力学，证明晶格完美时电子可自由通行，因而电导为无限，只有计入热运动与杂质才得到有限电导率。摘要并指出，在泡利原理下电导率与自由电子数不再成正比，只有费米分布临界能量附近的电子参与导电。
