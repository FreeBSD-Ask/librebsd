## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

科学交流与基础研究免受发表限制：

> Consistent with national policy and DOD Directives, DARPA fully supports scientific exchange.

> Our fundamental research awards are free from publication restrictions, export controls, and citizenship requirements.

未分类分包研究不受限制：

> We also place no restrictions on unclassified research assigned to subcontractors if the prime contractor and research performer determine the activity to be fundamental research, subject to certification by the contracting component.

必要时为国家安全施加限制：

> We do apply restrictions when necessary to safeguard national security, including preventing the disclosure of the performance characteristics of military systems as well as manufacturing technologies that are unique and critical to defense.

（同节另有一句界定判据：Each solicitation and award announcement states explicitly whether the activity constitutes fundamental research.）
