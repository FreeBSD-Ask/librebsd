# About the Digest — DragonFly BSD Digest（dragonflydigest.com/about/）

About the Digest This site provides a running record of what’s happening with DragonFly BSD. Most BSD projects tend to have very active mailing lists, which means wading through a lot of email messages just to stay up-to-date. The goal here is to provide a report of major and minor events for DragonFly, distilled down to the crucial facts and links. The site is maintained by Justin C. Sherrill.
