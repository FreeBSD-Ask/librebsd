## 正文摘录（逐字／近逐字，页面关键句）

"The Multics System Programmer's Manual (MSPM) was written by the Multics development team at MIT Project MAC, GE, and BTL between 1965 and about 1969."

"It was intended to be the primary design document for Multics."

"It ran to about 3000 pages in more than 800 chapters."

"Each chapter was carefully reviewed by peers, and many were rewritten or extensively revised several times."

"It is important to understand that the MSPM writing exercise was a step on the way to Multics, and that almost none of the design was implemented as written."

"But we would not have had as good a system without it: the MSPM represented a process innovation—balancing formality, writing things down, peer review, with imprecision, hiding details, and leaving specifics for later."

"Chapters were handwritten by programmers and typed by secretaries onto hectograph masters; blue hectograph copies were unapproved and temporary."

"MSPM occupied 10-12 four-inch three-ring binders, more than a shelf foot of standard office shelving, and maintaining the archive updates was a huge effort, so everyone's copy could differ."

"The scanned copy comes from Professor Jerry Saltzer's bookshelf (spring 2006)."

## 提要

multicians.org 的 MSPM 目录页记：《Multics 系统程序员手册》由 MIT Project MAC、GE 与贝尔实验室的 Multics 开发团队于 1965 至约 1969 年间撰写，意在成为 Multics 的首要设计文档；全书约 3000 页、逾 800 章，每章经同行仔细评审，许多章被多次重写或大幅修订。页面强调：MSPM 的写作练习是走向 Multics 的一步，其中几乎没有设计按原样实现；但若无它便不会有那样好的系统——MSPM 代表一种“过程创新”，在形式化、写下文档、同行评审与不精确、隐藏细节、留待日后之间取得平衡。页面并记：各章由程序员手写、秘书打字到胶版母版，蓝色胶版副本属未批准且临时；正式版经评审后重打、校对、制油印母版；MSPM 占 10—12 个四英寸三环活页夹，维护归档更新耗费巨大，因此每个人的副本都可能不同；现存扫描本取自 Jerry Saltzer 教授的书架（2006 年春）。
