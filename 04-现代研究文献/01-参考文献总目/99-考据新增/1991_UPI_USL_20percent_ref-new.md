Unix Systems Laboratories Inc., a computer software firm, sold a 20 percent equity stake Wednesday in a private placement and plans to become a public company in two to three years. …

Eleven different investors from three continents now own about 20 percent of Unix. AT&T, whose Bell Laboratories unit started Unix 23 years ago, still owns 70 percent and employees of Unix own the remaining 10 percent. …

Besides Motorola and ICL, the investing companies are: Amdahl Corp., of Sunnyvale, Calif.; Sun Microsystems Inc., Mountain View, Calif.; Novell Inc., Provo, Utah; Fujitsu Ltd., NEC Corp., OKI Electric Industry Co. Ltd., and Toshiba Corp., all of Tokyo; Institute for Information Industry of Taipei, Taiwan; and Ing. C. Olivetti & Co. SpA of Ivrea, Italy. …

Industry sources said AT&T had valued the company at about $325 million, which would translate into the new partners making a total investment of about $65 million.
