# 书评：Federal Communications Commission（Attorney General's Committee 行政程序专著第三号）引用卡

> 用途：V02.1.1.3“州际商委会管辖下的费率管制”第二来源（V02 补证批 b007）；曼恩—埃尔金斯法把 ICC 管辖扩及全部电报电话电缆线路，但该委员会少有行使。

## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

> The Mann-Elkins Act of 1910 extended the general regulatory jurisdiction of the Interstate Commerce Commission to all telegraph, telephone and cable lines, whether wire or wireless, which were engaged in interstate or foreign commerce.

> That Commission, however, as the monograph observes, found little occasion to exercise the wide powers conferred upon it.

> The Commission's jurisdiction was restricted, however, to radio, until the Communications Act of 1934 enlarged it to include all types of telegraph and telephone as well.

## 三、说明

书评转述司法部长行政程序委员会专著：1910 年曼恩—埃尔金斯法把 ICC 的一般监管管辖扩及一切从事州际或对外通商的电报、电话与电缆线路（不论有线无线）；但该委员会“少有场合行使”被授予的广泛权力；直到 1934 年《通信法》才把电报电话一并纳入 FCC 管辖。此与 NARA 档案沿革互证，并补出“权力存在而行使稀疏”的制度实况。
