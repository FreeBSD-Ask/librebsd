## 正文摘录（逐字）

"The Z1 was built using purely mechanical components; the Z3 used electromechanical relays. However, both machines shared a common logical structure, and their programming model was the same."

"The program was stored on punched tape and was read sequentially."

"the computing and control unit would be separated from the storage"

"Years before John von Neumann explained the advantages of a computer architecture in which the processor is separated from the memory, Zuse had already anived at the same solution."

"The instruction most conspicuously absent from the instruction set of the Z3 is conditional branching. Loops can be implemented by the simple expedient of bringing together the two ends of the punched tape, but there is no way to implement conditional sequences of instructions. The Z3 is therefore not a universal computer in the sense of Turing."

"The Z3 processor employs just 600 relays; the memory needs three times as much."

## 提要

Rojas 记：Z1（1936–1938）为纯机械、Z3（1941）为机电继电器，两机共享同一逻辑结构与编程模型；程序存于打孔带、顺序读取；存储与运算控制单元分离的思路早于 von Neumann 的阐述；Z3 处理器仅用 600 只继电器、内存为其三倍；指令集最显眼的缺席是条件转移——循环可由打孔带两端粘合实现，但无条件指令序列，故按图灵意义 Z3 不是通用计算机。
