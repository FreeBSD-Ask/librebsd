## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

> Its vehicle for doing so was the “Kingsbury commitment” of December 19, 1913, so named because it was expressed in a letter from AT&T Vice President Nathan C. Kingsbury to Attorney General McReynolds and Assistant Attorney General G. Carroll Todd of the Department of Justice.

> The Kingsbury letter committed AT&T to three things:

> 1) AT&T agreed to divest itself of its controlling stock holdings in its Western Union telegraph company, despite the important economies of scope gained from joint operation of telephone and telegraph lines; 2) AT&T agreed to stop acquiring competing independent exchanges, thus preserving dual service in the 1,234 cities and towns where Bell and an independent divided the market; 3) AT&T offered to open up its long-distance lines to independent exchanges under certain conditions.

> The Kingsbury commitment was neither a milestone nor a turning point but a brief pause on the road to regulated monopoly.

## 三、说明

书章给出承诺的三项内容（剥离西联控股、停止并购独立交换局、有条件开放长途线路）并判定其性质：“不是里程碑也不是转折点，而是通往受管制垄断路上的短暂停顿”。此与题面“以接受管制换取独立公司存续的承诺，确立受监管的私人垄断”直接对应，并提供批判性判读。
