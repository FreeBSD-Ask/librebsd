## 逐字引文池

- In a multi-CPU system, these functions change the interrupt priority level on the local CPU only.
- They are used by kernel code to block interrupts in critical sections, in order to protect data structures.
- The global kernel_lock is automatically acquired for interrupts at this level by default, in order to support device drivers that do not provide their own multiprocessor synchronization.
