## 正文摘录（逐字）

"To begin with, its members styled themselves as conceptual revolutionaries who cleared the stables of academic philosophy by showing metaphysics not simply to be false, but to be cognitively empty and meaningless."

"While the Vienna Circle’s early form of logical empiricism (or logical positivism or neopositivism: these labels will be used interchangeably here) no longer represents an active research program, recent history of philosophy of science has unearthed much previously neglected variety and depth in the doctrines of the Circle’s protagonists"

"The Vienna Circle’s theories were constantly changing."

## 提要

SEP 记：维也纳小组成员自视为概念上的革命者，以“形而上学不仅为假、且在认知上空洞无意义”清扫学院哲学；其早期逻辑经验主义（亦称逻辑实证主义或新实证主义）今已非活跃研究纲领，但科学哲学史研究重又发掘出其学说的多样与深度；小组理论本身不断变化。
