## 正文摘录（逐字，按全文块序拼接）

"Sometimes we talk of innovation in terms of 'technology push' and 'demand pull' to distinguish the source of new ideas. In those terms, ENIAC was created because of demand pull. The Computing Laboratory at the University of Pennsylvania was responsible for generating ranging tables for US Army artillery. They were getting further and further behind in their calculations. John Mauchley, Pres Eckert and their team, proposed developing an automatic calculating machine to speed up the process. Although the project was costly and risky, the Army approved."

"By the time they completed ENIAC, the urgency of computing ballistics tables occasioned by the war had passed. Thus when the developers introduced the machine to the public, they emphasized that it could also be used for many functions besides the task for which it had been created."

"During its lifetime, ENIAC solved about 100 unique problems. The computer's biggest user by far was the Computing Division of the Ballistics Research Laboratory, as might be expected. Seventy percent of the problems originated inside the Ballistics Research Laboratory. Fully 25% of the work ENIAC did was development of ballistics tables: the job it was originally designed to do."

## 提要

Smithsonian 的 ENIAC 用户口述史集记：以“技术推动／需求拉动”区分创新来源，ENIAC 属需求拉动——宾夕法尼亚大学计算实验室负责为美国陆军火炮生成射程表，计算积压日益严重，Mauchly、Eckert 及其团队提议造自动计算机加速该流程，陆军虽知项目昂贵且有风险仍予批准。该集并记：ENIAC 完成时，战争引起的弹道表计算紧迫性已过去，开发方对公众介绍机器时因此强调它还可用于原定任务之外的多种用途；在其服役期内 ENIAC 共解约一百个不同问题，最大用户为弹道研究实验室计算部，七成问题出自该实验室，全部工作中整整四分之一是弹道表编制——即它最初被设计来做的事。
