## 关键原文（逐字）

**§1 Terminological disambiguations（1.1.1 用）**：

> The term history is ambiguous. It is commonly used to refer both to the historical subject matter studied by historians and to the study of that subject matter. This ambiguity can easily generate confusion, since questions about historical events, processes, practices, ideas, institutions, and societies differ from questions about how they should be investigated and written about.

> The term history is also sometimes used synonymously with the past. These notions do not fully coincide. History itself is not confined to what is already past.

**§2 Main traditions 节引言（1.1.2 用）**：

> Analytical philosophy of history emerged in the twentieth century and was largely defined in opposition to its predecessor. Rather than seeking the meaning or direction of history as a whole, it focused on the conceptual and epistemological conditions of historical inquiry.

**导言（1.1.4、1.1.5 用）**：

> This approach, often called philosophy of historiography, examines the conceptual and epistemic conditions of the study of history and may therefore be regarded as a branch of philosophy of science. Its central questions concern historical knowledge, explanation, causation, laws, interpretation, narrative, representation, and the ethics of history writing.

> Much twentieth-century work was shaped by the analytical philosophy of history, while more recent discussions have expanded in multiple directions, including narrativist, postnarrativist, humanist and posthumanist, realist and irrealist, pragmatist as well as probabilistic and evidential approaches.

## 中文提要

Kuukkanen 现行词条以“历史哲学与史学哲学”分疏开篇：history 一词兼指题材与研究、又与 the past 不全重合；分析传统以对立于前身的方式界定自身；史学哲学的中心问题覆盖知识、解释、因果、叙事与写作伦理；晚近讨论扩展为叙事主义、后叙事主义、实在论与反实在论等多重方向。
