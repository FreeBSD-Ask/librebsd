## 正文摘录（逐字）

"When Microsoft signed the contract with IBM in November 1980, they had no such operating system. They too outsourced it, by first licensing then purchasing an operating system from Seattle Computer Products variously called QDOS"

"Microsoft proposed licensing “86-DOS”, which had been written by Tim Paterson at Seattle Computer Products (SCP) for their 8086-based computer kit because the 16-bit version of CP/M was late."

## 提要

CHM 记：微软 1980 年 11 月与 IBM 签约时并无自己的操作系统，先取得授权、后整体购入西雅图计算机产品公司（SCP）名为 QDOS 的系统；微软提议授权由蒂姆·帕特森为 SCP 8086 套件所写的“86-DOS”，起因是 CP/M 的 16 位版本迟迟未出。两条分别承 4.5.4 的系统转手与 4.5.6 的授权（而非随硬件附送）模式。
