## 正文摘录（逐字）

"Others attempted to build difference engines in Babbage's time, all with dismal outcomes."

"Inspired by an account of Babbage's first engine, published in 1834, a Swedish father-and-son team, Georg and Edvard Scheutz, built a working prototype, completed in 1843."

"Two fully engineered versions in metal followed, the first in 1853 in Stockholm, the second in 1859 in London for the General Register Office."

"Neither was much of a success. The hoped-for economies of automatic tabulation by machinery did not materialize."

"Worse still, the machine, made in London, lacked Babbage's security devices, tended to derangement, and required constant care."

"Both father and son died bankrupt. Building difference engines contributed to their ruin."

"The three engines now reside in museums, handsome testaments to dashed hopes and financial disaster."

## 提要

Computer History Museum 记：巴贝奇同时代另有人试造差分机，结局皆不佳；瑞典的 Georg 与 Edvard Scheutz 父子受 1834 年发表的巴贝奇首台引擎报道启发，1843 年完成可工作的原型，其后造出两台全工程化金属机，第一台 1853 年在斯德哥尔摩，第二台 1859 年在伦敦为 General Register Office 制造；两机均不算成功，自动制表所期望的经济性未实现，伦敦制造的一台缺少巴贝奇的安全装置、易于失调且需持续照料；父子二人去世时均已破产，造机是致其破产的原因之一；三台机器现存于博物馆。
