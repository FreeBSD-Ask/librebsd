## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

政—产—学互补关系（4.4.4 采用反馈的制度前提）：

> Innovation in computing stems from a complementary relationship among government, industry, and universities.

商业发展反哺政府事业（4.4.4 反馈回路之主句）：

> Commercial developments, similarly, have contributed to government endeavors.

联邦资助与产业投入互补而非替代（4.4.6 必要性而非充分性）：

> Primary among them is that federal support for research has tended to complement, rather than preempt, industry investments in research.

历史方法的反事实限度（4.4.5 模型边界）：

> history can show the influence of federal funding on historical innovations in computing, but it cannot suggest what directions might have been taken without federal support.

人事与经费并列的影响因素（4.4.5 边界情形成因）：

> Past outcomes are often tied to specific circumstances. The success or failure of specific research programs, for example, may be influenced as much by the particular people involved as by the amount of funding available.

分寸用语（贡献而非决定）：

> federal funding of major computing initiatives has often contributed substantially to the development and deployment of commercial technologies.

## 三、考据说明

1. 报告将信息革命定性为“社会技术革命”，主张政府—产业—大学三方互补，是“资助—产出”线性叙事最主要的方法论修正来源；其 Executive Summary 明言历史不能回答“若无联邦支持会怎样”，可直接支撑 4.4.5 的边界讨论。
2. 4.4.6 之“必要／充分／触发”三分，与本报告“complement, rather than preempt”句及“often contributed substantially”的分寸用语互证：报告自身即避免把资助写成充分条件。
3. 与 Denning 1989（ref1554）合用：报告给制度层判断，Denning 给 1969–1989 网络演进的过程层例证（NCP→TCP/IP 之重写由使用增长触发）。
4. 禁引核查：国家科学院出版社官方开放全文，`banned_source=0`；按 L3 纪律引用（学术记载，非当事人证词）。

## 四、补充引文（b025 取用，Executive Summary 章内）

资助额增长曲线（3.1.2 时段变化）：

> From less than $10 million in 1960, federal funding for research in computer science climbed to almost $1 billion in 1995.

大学研究资助份额（3.1.3 金额与方向）：

> Federal support has constituted roughly 70 percent of total university research funding in computer science and electrical engineering since 1976.

核心机构与人才培养（3.1.4 核心节点）：

> In computer science departments at universities such as the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), Stanford University, the University of California at Berkeley (UC-Berkeley), and Carnegie Mellon University, over half of all graduate students receive financial support from the federal government, mostly in the form of research assistantships.

数据出处（3.1.5 史料来源）：

> Source: Federal research funding from NSF (1998b), Table 25; industry research funding compiled from the 1979-1998 editions of the annual National Science Foundation report Research and Development in Industry.

量化限度（3.1.6 方法边界）：

> The effects of federal support for computing research are difficult to quantify but pervasive.

## 五、补充引文（b035 取用，Chapter 3 与 Chapter 5 章页）

大学研究经费对联邦的依赖（1.2.2 受助机构资助结构）：

> Since 1973, federal agencies have provided roughly 70 percent of all funding for university research in computer science.

IPTO 成立两年内 DARPA 成为计算机科学研究的最大资助者（1.2.2 资助来源集中度）：

> within 2 years of establishing its Information Processing Techniques Office in 1962, the Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (DARPA) became the dominant source of funding, providing more support for computer science research than all other federal agencies combined.

大计划—多机构—卓越中心（1.2.1 项目制科研的组织形态）：

> DARPA, for example, has tended to award contracts for large programs involving multiple researchers and research organizations. It has concentrated its funding for computer research on a limited number of centers of excellence, such as MIT, Stanford University, Carnegie Mellon University, and UC-Berkeley.

项目经理的遴选裁量（1.2.5 项目遴选与方向设定）：

> Program managers have generally been given significant discretion in selecting and shaping new research initiatives.

两级批准制（1.2.3 高自主性的制度条件）：

> Especially in the 1960s and 1970s, DARPA gave program managers sufficient funds to shape coherent research programs, and program budgets required only two levels of approval: one by the office director and one by the DARPA director.

研究者的智识自由（1.2.3 自主性的边界与弹性）：

> Researchers need sufficient intellectual freedom to follow their intuition and to modify research plans based on preliminary results.

按某些尺度失败的项目（1.2.6 高风险研究的容错）：

> By some measures, Project Whirlwind and SAGE were failures (see Chapter 4).

未达原定目标仍有产出（1.2.6 容错的知识产出）：

> even research projects that do not achieve their original objectives can produce meaningful results or generate valuable knowledge for guiding future research efforts.

大学研究的长期取向（1.2.2 受助机构的自主性来源）：

> Because they are not usually subject to commercial pressures, university researchers often have greater ability than their industrial counterparts to explore ideas with uncertain long-term payoffs.

弹性须由人与文化共同承担（1.2.3 条件的互补性）：

> Building such flexibility into federal structures for managing research requires both skilled program managers—who understand, articulate, and promote the visions of researchers—and an organizational culture that accepts and promotes exploratory efforts.

## 七、补充引文（b037 取用，Chapter 4 与 Chapter 5）

大学实验室的承接（1.4.1 大学实验室的承接方式）：

> In 1956, at the request of the Air Force, the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) created Project Lincoln, now known as the Lincoln Laboratory, with a broad charter to study problems in air defense to protect the nation from nuclear attack.

大计划中的企业名单（1.4.2 企业实验室的参与路径）：

企业的收益位置（1.4.2 参与的回报形态）：

合作研制与量产（1.4.5 合同安排下的协作产物）：

> The Air Force subsequently purchased production versions of the computer (designed in a cooperative effort between MIT and IBM) to equip each of its 23 Direction Centers.

人员投入规模（1.4.6 人员、设备与场地的投入）：

> At its peak, between 7,000 and 8,000 IBM employees worked on the project.

大学与企业的资助配比（1.4.4 三类机构的分工与互补）：

研究与系统研制的配比（1.4.4 分工的另一轴）：

设备投入的联邦份额（1.4.6 设备投入）：

> Between 1981 and 1995, the federal government provided between 59 and 76 percent of annual research equipment expenditures in computer science and between 64 and 83 percent of annual research equipment expenditures in electrical engineering.
