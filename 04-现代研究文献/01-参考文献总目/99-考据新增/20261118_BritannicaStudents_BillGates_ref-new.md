## b007 引文（2026-11-18 逐字录入）

Gates licensed the operating system MS-DOS to the International Business Machines Corporation (IBM) for use in its first personal computer, which appeared in 1981. From then on, all IBM personal computers carried Microsoft software. Other companies soon began making personal computers, and they also depended on Microsoft software.
