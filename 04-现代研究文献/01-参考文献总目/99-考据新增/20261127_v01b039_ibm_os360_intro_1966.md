## 正文摘录（逐字）

"An important feature of an operating system is its ability to process a continuous series of jobs with little or no operator intervention. By reducing the degree of human participation in the mechanics of data processing, the operating system ensures that jobs are processed faster and are less subject to human error."

## 提要

IBM 1966 年 11 月《System/360 操作系统：导论》记：操作系统的一个重要特性是能在很少或没有操作员干预下处理连续作业序列；通过减少人在数据处理机械过程中的参与，系统使作业处理更快、更少受人为错误影响。该册把“操作员退出、作业流自动运转”写成系统设计目标，是 4.1.1 的同期官方依据。
