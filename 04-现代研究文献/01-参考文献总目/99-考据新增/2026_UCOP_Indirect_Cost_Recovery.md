## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

间接成本的构成：

> Indirect costs — sometimes called overhead, facilities and administrative (F&A) costs, or shared expenses — are costs incurred in the conduct of externally sponsored research that are shared across a large number of projects as well as other functions of the University.

按联邦协商费率计收：

> The university seeks to apply its federally negotiated F&A rates to all extramurally funded projects. These rate agreements are developed and negotiated based on Uniform Guidance (2 CFR 200), the federal government's regulation that provides cost principles for federal awards.

## 三、说明

大学系统官方说明确认：间接成本为跨项目共享的设施与管理支出；大学对全部外部资助项目适用联邦协商的 F&A 费率，费率依 2 CFR 200 协商确定。与 eCFR 条文互为独立来源。
