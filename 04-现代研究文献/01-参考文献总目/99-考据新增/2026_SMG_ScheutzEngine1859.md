## 正文摘录（逐字）

"Scheutz difference engine, made by Bryan Donkin & Co. in 1859"

"Much of Victorian science, engineering and commerce made use of tables of numbers, calculated by hand by people known as 'computers'. It could be mind-numbing work."

"This 'difference engine', invented by the mathematician Charles Babbage and brought to market by Georg and Edvard Scheutz, was an attempt to mechanise the process, reducing errors which could cost lives in applications such as navigation."

"It was bought by the British government to calculate figures for the English Life Table of 1864."

"This mammoth publication included life expectancies of different parts of the population, and was used by insurance companies to price their premiums."

## 提要

Science Museum Group 藏品记录记：该舍茨差分机为第三型，1859 年由伦敦的 Bryan Donkin & Co. 制造，藏于科学博物馆 Winton 数学展厅（藏品号 1914-122/1）。维多利亚时代的科学、工程与商业大量依赖人称 “computers” 的人工计算编制数表；这台差分机由巴贝奇发明、舍茨父子推向市场，意在机械化制表流程、减少在航海等应用中可能致命的错误。英国政府购机用于计算 1864 年《English Life Table》的数据，该出版物含不同人群的预期寿命，保险公司据以厘定保费。

## 存疑与边界

- 藏品记录未载购机价格与交付日期；1859 年与 Computer History Museum 的“1859 年在伦敦为 General Register Office 制造”一致。
- “brought to market” 为博物馆对舍茨父子角色的表述，不含商业成败判断。
