## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译）

联邦机构须接受协商费率：

> Negotiated indirect cost rates must be accepted by all Federal agencies.

高等教育机构间接成本的两类划分：

> For major Institutions of Higher Education (IHE) and major nonprofit organizations, indirect costs must be classified within two broad categories: "Facilities" and "Administration."

费率提案与确定依据：

> Appendix III to Part 200—Indirect (F&A) Costs Identification and Assignment, and Rate Determination for Institutions of Higher Education (IHEs)

## 三、说明

本条确立联邦资助中间接成本（设施与管理，F&A）的识别、分摊与费率确定规则，并规定联邦机构须接受协商费率；高等教育机构适用 Appendix III。属现行统一指南（Uniform Guidance）条文。
