## 二、逐字引文（英文，未翻译；右单引号 U+2019 与 en dash 照录）

离任与交接（2.2.1B，ARPA→Telenet）：

> I had announced that I was leaving, but I couldn't find anybody to take my place at ARPA.

ARPA 社区的合作面（2.2.2A）：

> anybody in the computer field, Tom and I and everybody else, worked within the whole ARPA community – not with the communications people.

制度性施压（2.2.3A，林肯实验室主任转述 IPTO 以资助比例施压）：

> I think it's in our best interest that you go. They've told me that they have 51% of my funding, and I'll support you going down.

被挖者的回顾（2.2.5A）：

> And, in fact, he did. He helped convince me that it was in my best interest to get into management more and do this, but they did that to me, and it worked.

当事人记忆与档案查证（2.2.6A）：

> I'm going to leave that to your reference checking. I don't know the year.

研究方向的共识（2.2.4B）：

> All of us in computing were clearly not going to go after it on a circuit switched basis.

## 三、考据说明

1. Roberts 为 IPTO 主任（1968—1973），其访谈完整记述被 Taylor 招募（1966）、离任 ARPA 与出任 Telenet 总裁（1973-10-01）的路径，是 2.2 节“流动路径”“制度空间”两题的直接一手。
2. “51% of my funding”句为 Roberts 转述林肯实验室主任的话，是资助机构以经费杠杆促成人员借调的罕见证词；正文表述须注明“据当事人转述”。
3. L4 纪律：事后追述的年份与细节（如 1966 年底赴华盛顿、1973-10-01 入职 Telenet）须与档案互证；与 Kahn 2006 访谈合用构成双一手（不同当事人、不同访谈）。
