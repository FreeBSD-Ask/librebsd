 YouTube Creator Awards are our way of recognizing the extraordinary effort creators put into their growing channels and to build thriving communities, responsibly. To be eligible for a Creator Award, creators need to meet the eligibility criteria and must follow our policies. 

   YouTube Creator Awards   

Subscribe to the  YouTube Creators channel &nbsp;for the latest news, updates, and tips. 

 Find out if your channel is eligible for a YouTube Creator Award, and get your award redemption code if you are: 

    Check Your Creator Award Eligibility   

 Eligibility criteria 

   To be eligible for a Creator Award, you need to meet the following guidelines: 

     Pass a specific subscriber milestone:

         Silver: When you reach 100,000 subscribers 
         Gold: When you reach 1,000,000 subscribers 
         Diamond: When you reach 10,000,000 subscribers 
         Red Diamond: When you reach 100,000,000 subscribers 

     Your channel is  active  (has uploads within the last 6 months). 
     You don’t have an active  Community Guidelines  violation, and haven’t received any in the last 365 days. 
     You don’t have active copyright strikes or violations. 
     You’re in compliance with YouTube’s  Terms of Service . 
     You haven’t been rejected or suspended from the  YouTube Partner Program . 
     Your channel isn’t terminated or linked to an account that has been terminated. 
     You don’t have content that is  deceptive, spammy, or a scam . 
     Your main content is original. Channels that focus on compilations, mixes, curation, or heavy use of someone else's copyrighted content or characters may be ineligible. 

   You don’t have to be a member of the YouTube Partner Program (YPP) to be eligible for an award. Creator Awards are given at YouTube's discretion. Each channel is carefully reviewed to make sure the criteria is met. 

   Creator Awards are for personal use only, and may not be sold or distributed to anyone outside of your channel team members. If you violate this policy, you may be subject to disciplinary action. 

   Disciplinary actions can include: 

     Forfeiture of the award 
     Ineligibility for future awards 
     Possible termination of your YouTube or Google account 

      Check out our comprehensive FAQs  around YouTube Creator Awards for more info. 

    Country/region availability  

   The YouTube Creator Awards program is unavailable for channels in the following countries/regions: 

           Belarus (BY) 
           Cuba (CU) 
           Iran (IR) 
           Myanmar (MM) 
           North Korea (KP) 

           Russia (RU) 
           Somalia (SO) 
           Sudan (SD) 
           Syria (SY) 
           Tajikistan (TJ) 

           Vanuatu (VU) 
           Venezuela (VE) 
           Yemen (YE) 

 Redeem a YouTube Creator Award 

   Once you’ve met our award criteria, we will send you a redemption code. Follow these steps to redeem your award: 

     Find and copy the redemption code we sent you.

         If you’ve misplaced that redemption code ,  visit our  interactive eligibility check  to get your redemption code. 

     Go to the  Creator Awards redemption website  and enter your code. 
     Enter your shipping info and specify how you want your channel name to appear on the award. 
     Select “Order Now” to finish redeeming your award. 

       If you’re located in Bangladesh, Brazil, India, Indonesia, Mexico, Saudi Arabia, South Korea, Turkey, and Vietnam, you’ll be asked for extra info such as a tax ID when redeeming your Creator Award. The relevant customs offices require this info. 

 Shipping and delivery 

   After you submit your shipping info and redeem your award, we’ll deliver it to you within approx. 2-3 weeks. Orders placed after June 1, 2023, can be tracked  here . To make sure your award gets to you on time, note the following: 

     Don’t forget to use your full legal name when submitting shipping info. This helps in case you have to pick it up from a customs or delivery office. 
     You’ll get an automated email from the shipping company containing a tracking number once your award has shipped. Sign up for tracking notifications to ensure your award reaches you successfully. 
     You’re responsible to get back to any contact made by a delivery company that requests more documentation or info from you. This may help your award pass through customs or be delivered to you. 
     You’re responsible to pay any customs duties and/or taxes that are required in certain countries/regions. Legally, YouTube can’t cover these costs. These countries include, but are not limited to: Armenia, Azerbaijan, Cambodia, Kyrgyzstan, and Uzbekistan. 
     If you don’t get back to a delivery company, your Creator Award may be returned or lost. In these cases, you may have to purchase a replacement if you want to get your Creator Award. 

       Once received, you have seven days to report any damage to Society Awards by submitting  this form . You will need to send photos of the award and the box, so please keep all packaging materials. This will also allow you to return the award to the vendor for a replacement. Please note that a complimentary replacement will only be granted once the original award is returned and evaluated. 

     For any shipping related issues or concerns, get in touch with our production team  here .
