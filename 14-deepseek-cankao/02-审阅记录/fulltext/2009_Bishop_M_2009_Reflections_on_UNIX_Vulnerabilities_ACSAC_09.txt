					 Hear from members of the Reflections Executive Team 				 

									 Dr William Hanna, Reflections Executive Clinical Director, oversees all aspects of Clinical care for all of Reflections clients. From initial assessment, clinical diagnoses, treatment plan creation, clinical progress monitoring through to discharge and continuing care planning, a critical component of the client&#8217;s treatment plan. 									Reflections treatment program is focused on the underlying mental health co-occurring disorders at the root cause of the substance use disorder. While minimal medication is the goal we also recognise the need that, for certain disorders, an optimized and effective medication regime iis necessary. 				Why Choose Us?				 

					 Residential Treatment 				 

									 Reflections Residential Treatment Facilities are State of California Licensed and Joint Commission Accredited treatment programs offering Clinically managed medication assisted withdrawal (sub acute detoxification) and high intensity treatment of substance use disorders and co-occurring disorders such as Trauma, Anxiety and Depression, PTSD, Grief and Loss, Undiagnosed Bipolar and Borderline personality disorder and many more. Our intensive broad spectrum of clinicians include Ph.d Licensed Clinical Psychologists; Psychiatrists, LMFT&#8217;s, Drug and Alcohol Certified Counselors and Adjunctive Therapists including Music, Art, Equine, Massage, and Meditation. 								 

									 Additionally Reflections values the insight and collaboration of clients&#8217; families and loved ones in order to incorporate private family sessions into the client&#8217;s treatment program in order to ensure all involved are aligned and that there is long term success of the family unit.   We also welcome the input and, where appropriate, the collaboration with external care providers that the clients are already working with including individual therapists, psychiatrists, primary care providers and attorneys.   Reflections provides the highest level of intensive and individualized treatment plans in a solution focused, empowerment and strengths based approach. We respect the importance of continued contact with healthy external activities (work, family, school etc) and clients have full access to electronics (laptops, cell phones etc) learning to balance all aspects of their lives.

				Frequently Asked Questions				 

					 FAQs &amp; More 				 

									 Our addiction and mental health recovery programs can be integrated into your life anywhere within the continuum of care. Our flexible services provide convenient options for sustaining recovery online, on your schedule. 								 

												 What is Dual Diagnosis Treatment?	 

					  Dual diagnosis treatment is the concurrent treatment of Substance Use Disorders (SUD) and Mental Health (MH). Reflections focuses heavily on the treatment of MH as it generally is the root cause leading to SUD.  

												 What are the credentials of my Treatment Team?	 

					  Your treatment team consists of high caliber, seasoned treatment professionals. Reflections provides Doctorate level clinicians, Masters level clinicians, Certified Addiction counselors.  

												 Is my family involved in treatment?	 

					  Yes. Whoever is in your immediate support group in your family will likely be involved in family sessions. The frequency and appropriateness of who should be involved will be a collaborative decision based on the clinical team&#8217;s recommendations and the readiness of the client.  

												 How long is the treatment?	 

					  Reflection individualized treatment programs can vary from 30 days up to 90 days. The length of treatment is determined by the client and their clinical team based on progress in treatment and the completion of a solid continuing care plan.

				Begin your Recovery at Reflections				 

					 Our Philosophy 				 

									Our philosophy is to help our patients heal not only physically, but also mentally, and spiritually, so that when they leave our facility they are able to live a full, healthy life, without relapse.

									   Dual-Diagnosis Treatment  Reflections is a Dual Diagnosis treatment program addressing the co-occurring mental health and substance use disorders. Dual diagnosis treatment is a multi faceted intensive and individualized treatment approach led by credentialed and seasoned mental health professionals including intensive psychotherapy of varying modalities, family therapy, substance use counseling ,and multiple adjunctive and wellness modalities.  								 

									   Alumni Program  Clients who have graduated from Reflections become life long connections and friends staying in contact  through regular checkins, newsletters and social media. Additionally Reflections offers a monthly Alumni zoom meeting hosted by Reflections Executive Director Olejan Hafstad with regular topic discussions and guest speakers including Reflections staff, specialists  in the field and alumni .These take place on the second Tuesday of every month at 7pm.       								 

 	  Family Involvement 
 Reflections values the engagement of family and loved ones during the course of treatment to ensure the best chances of long term successs for all involved. The clients Primary Therapist will reach out to loved ones in the early days of treatment and, if clinically recommended, private family sessions will be incorporated into the clients individualized treatment program. 

 Additionally, once per month, clients and family members are invited to a one day educational workshop hosted br Dr William Hanna, Refectons Executive Clinical Director and Olejan Hafstad, Executive Director.
