# 311.2.5 当事人素材与叙述章——BSD vs GPL 的哲学差异（2004–2025）

本章 claim 取自 dslinux_claims.duckdb；该库不含 chapter 字段，故以 copyleft、GPL、BSDL 与内核许可证标签治理为关键词选取相关记录，并按邮件日期自 2004 年排至 2025 年

分歧的技术内核朴素得近乎刺眼：BSDL 全文没有一处要求分发修改后的对应源代码，GPL §5 则明确要求，于是同一段代码，在 BSD 下作者可以把二进制交给你而把源代码留在自己抽屉里，在 GPL 下他做不到，条款的这一处空缺成了此后二十年所有争论的支点

最早被点破的是这条差异的现实代价，2004 年 9 月 13 日 Matthias Andree 在 linux-scsi 上看见 Matthew Wilcox 将 sym 驱动 Relicence from dual BSD/GPL to pure GPL，当即警告 The BSD world ain't gonna like this for practical reasons, the consequence is that they'll either stop merging patches from Linux or remove the sym driver from their GENERIC kernels if they want your patches——许可证不是纸面文字，它会切断两条内核树之间已经建立的补丁流转

两年内民间直觉被压缩到一行字以内：2005 年 8 月 Adaptec 的 Mark Salyzyn 写下 GPL infects, BSD relieves stomach ulcers；10 月 Arjan van de Ven 审查 qla2xxx 许可变更时判断，代码身处内核源码包时只有 GPL 条款有效，the BSD part of it is "dormant" until someone takes the driver outside the linux kernel context

2007 年 7 月，Jeff Garzik 追问 Wilcox 为何擅改 advansys 许可：What do the other copyright holders think about this? 对方答 They're not contactable，Garzik 反驳 AFAIK that does not eliminate your legal obligation，单一授权权与多版权人共治之间的张力，在 BSD 一侧由分散持有的版权天然吸收，在 GPL 一侧却须逐案面对

到 2012 年 11 月，GPL §5 的作用被最直白地复述，Andy Grover 论 scsi target 与 open core 时说 RTS is trying to use an "open core" business model. This works fine for BSD-licensed code or code originally authored entirely by you, but their code (all of it even the new stuff) is a derivative work of the Linux kernel source code, and the GPL says they need to contribute it back——BSD 不禁止这种商业模式，GPL 在衍生作品条款下禁止，而 open core 恰是两种自由观正面对撞的地面

2015 年 7 月 4 日，David Woodhouse 在 ksummit-discuss 上发起 [CORE TOPIC] GPL enforcement actions，为这场争论立起最诚实的坐标系：Some parties are portrayed as acting excessively and inappropriately — and with dubious motives — to enforce the GPL; others as being rabidly *against* enforcement and effectively wanting a BSD-licensed free-for-all because that supports their corporate interests，他随即补上 None of these portrayals are entirely accurate, I'm sure, and the histrionics don't really do anyone any good

2016 年 8 月关于 sections 与链接表的许可之争，给出了这场分歧最精确的技术法律表述，Alan Cox 先说模块与内核的组合体始终按更严格者计算，which is always GPLv2 because the stricter licence rules，继而点出 BSDL 无 copyleft 的字面后果：Because if I build a BSD licensed module against the kernel, give you the binaries and refuse to give you the source I am conforming to the BSD licence in letter. So to use it with the kernel it needs to be GPL with additional rights (eg BSD including the source...)

同日 James Bottomley 拆开这层结构：I can produce a Linux kernel module under a pure BSD licence because BSD is compatible with GPL. However, if we assume for the sake of argument that a binary module is a derived work of Linux, producing and distributing the binary for the module brings the combination under GPLv2 via the derivative works clause and I'm required to offer you corresponding source code in spite of the fact that my module *only* has a BSD licence，他把 BSDL 的自由边界界定得极为清楚：I can create a pure BSD module ... I'm still free to create binary only versions under BSD (as long as they're for something other than inserting into Linux). However, if I want my binary only modules to be combined with Linux, I have to follow GPLv2 compliance——自由在于不受他人约束，代价在于不能把这份自由延伸到组合体，BSDL 的宽松与 GPL 的传染因此在此完成了一次精确的对表

Cox 的治理回应同样锋利：内核之所以不设 BSD 标签，We don't want anyone to create a module under your licence, claim it is "open source" then fifteen years later release the module in binary only form ... It's the same reason we don't have a BSD tag but use "GPL with additional rights" to stop abuse of the module tags；Bottomley 反驳 We do have separate Dual licence tags. Dual BSD/GPL allows me to do this today, without waiting fifteen years

2021 年 4 月，rust-for-linux 上 Mariusz Ceier 以 IANAL 开场转引 LWN 访谈，把 BSD 宽松许可的长期后果压缩成一次经验观察：I've seen a lot of BSD (or MIT or similar) licensed open source projects that just fragment when they become big enough to be commercially important, and the involved companies inevitably decide to turn their own parts proprietary；So I think the GPLv2 is pretty much the perfect balance of "everybody works under the same rules", and still requires that people give back to the community ("tit-for-tat")

2022 年 7 月的 linux-man 争论最接近哲学本身，Branden Robinson 断言 Under this umbrella, the Linux kernel is effectively under the BSD license，Ingo Schwarze 反驳 Except that free software projects cannot copy from it - that's quite a big BUT since allowing *everybody* to copy the code for any purpose is the central idea of the BSD license；面对 The BSD camp did ultimately win the copyleft argument after all，Schwarze 答 I'm not so sure about that，理由是 GPL 保留的权利超出伯尔尼公约本已不可让渡的范畴，Even if in effect, the Copyleft aspect of the GPL is not usually enforced against Foundation members, GPL code is far from as free as the Berne Convention would permit it to be, and far from as free as if it were under a BSD license

Colomar 用一句非对称总结这场对峙：the kernel is BSD-licensed to big corporations paying to the FSF, but GPL to free software programmers. BSDs are BSD for everyone，他自评 I'm not accepting the lose-lose situation，认为 GPL 实践上未达成 RMS 的设计目标却留下 unintended detrimental side effects，结尾留下几乎超越许可证的一句 the intention matters more than the license to me

两个月后 Miguel Ojeda 用双许可做务实折中：The DRM core is dual-licensed GPL/MIT, as are some of the major drivers (i915/radeon/amdgpu at least), and this allows OpenBSD to also use this code directly——同一份代码同时承载两种自由观，是分歧在工程上最常见的和解形式

2025 年 10 月，Robinson 在 AI 贡献政策争论中把分歧推回贡献者准入层面：projects that employ copyleft licenses foreclose contributions from people who hold that BSD-style licenses, or dedication to the public domain, are "the only true free[dom]"；12 月 30 日他又回指起源，Berkeley CSRG 最后一次发布为 4.4BSD Lite Release 2，日期 1995 年 5 月，此后 The "real work" was by then happening as BSDI, where every developer was assured that their deeply principled avoidance of the viral GPL would result in billionaire status for them all. Winning!——BSD 阵营回避 GPL，从来既是伦理姿态也是商业策略

二十余年列表记录显示，这场分歧既不纯属理念之争亦不纯属善恶之争，而是利益分配方案之别，BSD 主张作者保有把二进制与源码分离的自由，GPL 主张组合体的每位使用者都保有获得源码的自由，前者接近消极自由，后者接近积极自由，Woodhouse 那句 None of these portrayals are entirely accurate，至今仍是两侧各自的镜子
