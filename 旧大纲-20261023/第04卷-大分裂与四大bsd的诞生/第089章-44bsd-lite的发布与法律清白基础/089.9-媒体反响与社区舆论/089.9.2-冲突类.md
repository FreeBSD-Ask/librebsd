# 089.9.2 冲突类

### 新闻条目 1

- **新闻标题（中英）**：Twenty Years of Berkeley Unix: From AT&T-Owned to Freely Redistributable — Kirk McKusick's account of USL lawsuit and 4.4BSD-Lite release（伯克利 Unix 二十年：从 AT&T 拥有到自由再分发 —— Kirk McKusick 对 USL 诉讼与 4.4BSD-Lite 发布的陈述）
- **新闻来源**：O'Reilly Openbook（开源文集《Open Sources: Voices from the Open Source Revolution》）+ Marshall Kirk McKusick（马歇尔·柯克·麦库西克） + 1999-01（第一版） + **<https://www.oreilly.com/openbook/opensources/book/kirkmck.html>** + 访问日期 2026-07-27
- **新闻摘要**：Kirk McKusick 在 O'Reilly 出版的开源文集章节中亲述 USL 诉讼与 4.4BSD-Lite 发布：“In December 1992, Dickinson R. Debevoise, a United States District Judge in New Jersey, heard the arguments for the injunction... On a Friday about six weeks later, he issued a forty-page opinion in which he denied the injunction and threw out all but two of the complaints.“关于和解：”Soon after the filing in state court, USL was bought from AT&T by Novell. The CEO of Novell, Ray Noorda, stated publicly that he would rather compete in the marketplace than in court. By the summer of 1993, settlement talks had started... a settlement was finally reached in January 1994. The result was that three files were removed from the 18,000 that made up Networking Release 2, and a number of minor changes were made to other files. In addition, the University agreed to add USL copyrights to about 70 files, although those files continued to be freely redistributed.“关于 4.4BSD-Lite 发布：”The newly blessed release was called 4.4BSD-Lite and was released in June 1994 under terms identical to those used for the Networking releases.“关于 CSRG 解散：”Following the release of 4.4BSD-Lite Release 2, the CSRG was disbanded. After nearly twenty years of piloting the BSD ship, we felt that it was time to let others with fresh ideas and boundless enthusiasm take over.”
- **与本章主题的关联说明**：本章核心为 4.4BSD-Lite 发布与 USL 诉讼和解，Kirk McKusick 作为 CSRG 核心成员与 USL 诉讼当事人，其亲述是 4.4BSD-Lite 事件最权威的一手史料，构成本章叙事的骨架。McKusick 关于“1994-01 和解、1994-06 4.4BSD-Lite 发布、1995-06 4.4BSD-Lite Release 2 发布、随后 CSRG 解散”的时序构成本章时间线锚点。
- **与官方史料的交叉验证结论**：McKusick 关于“1994-01 和解”的陈述与 FreeBSD Handbook 1.3.1 节“Novell 和 U.C. Berkeley 决定就 Berkeley Net/2 磁带的归属权问题打的那场马拉松式的官司”叙事一致；关于“1994-06 4.4BSD-Lite 发布”的陈述与 FreeBSD Handbook 1.3.1 节“FreeBSD then set about the arduous task of literally re-inventing itself from a completely new and rather incomplete set of 4.4BSD-Lite bits”叙事一致；关于“1995-06 4.4BSD-Lite Release 2 发布、随后 CSRG 解散”的陈述与 FreeBSD Handbook 1.3.1 节“FreeBSD 2.0.5 release in June of 1995”时序一致。

### 新闻条目 2

- **新闻标题（中英）**：Explaining BSD — The USL lawsuit and BSD/OS history（解释 BSD —— USL 诉讼与 BSD/OS 历史）
- **新闻来源**：FreeBSD Project 官方文档 + Greg Lehey 撰写 + 持续维护至 2026 年 + **<https://www.freebsd.org/doc/en_US.ISO8859-1/articles/explaining-bsd/>** + 访问日期 2026-07-27
- **新闻摘要**：FreeBSD 官方文档“Explaining BSD”第 3 节“Why is BSD not better known?”明确记载 USL 诉讼对 BSD 知名度的影响：“In 1992, AT&T sued BSDI, the vendor of BSD/386, alleging that the product contained AT&T-copyrighted code. The case was settled out of court in 1994, but the spectre of the litigation continues to haunt people. In March 2000 an article published on the web claimed that the court case had been ‘recently settled’.“文档同时记载 4.4BSD-Lite 发布与 BSD/OS 关系：”BSD/OS was the oldest of the 4.4BSD derivatives. It was not open source, though source code licenses were available at relatively low cost. It resembled FreeBSD in many ways. Two years after the acquisition of BSDi by Wind River Systems, BSD/OS failed to survive as an independent product.”
- **与本章主题的关联说明**：本章 4.4BSD-Lite 发布事件须包含 USL 诉讼对 BSD 阵营长期影响的陈述，FreeBSD 官方“Explaining BSD”文档是 FreeBSD 阵营对 USL 诉讼长期影响的当事人叙事，构成本章 BSD 阵营对 USL 诉讼长期影响立场锚点。文档中“spectre of the litigation continues to haunt people”陈述显示 USL 诉讼对 BSD 阵营知名度的长期负面影响。
- **与官方史料的交叉验证结论**：文档关于“1992 年 AT&T 起诉 BSDI、1994 年庭外和解”的陈述与 McKusick 亲述“In December 1992... a settlement was finally reached in January 1994”一致（时点略有差异：McKusick 1992-12 为禁令听证，1994-01 为和解，Explaining BSD 1992 为起诉、1994 为和解）；关于“In March 2000 an article published on the web claimed that the court case had been ‘recently settled’”的陈述显示 2000 年仍有媒体对 USL 诉讼时点的误报，须按 `00-编纂规则与格式规范/01-规则/考据规则.md` 标注“媒体误报”。

### 新闻条目 3

- **新闻标题（中英）**：The unknown hackers — Rachel Chalmers on USL lawsuit's impact on 386BSD fragmentation（鲜为人知的黑客 —— Rachel Chalmers 论 USL 诉讼对 386BSD 分化的影响）
- **新闻来源**：Salon + Rachel Chalmers + 2000-05-17 + **<https://www.salon.com/2000/05/17/386bsd/>** + 访问日期 2026-07-27
- **新闻摘要**：Rachel Chalmers 在 Salon 长篇专访中记载 USL 诉讼对 386BSD 用户社区分化的影响：“By the time 1.0 was released, the x86BSD user community had fragmented. Some developers had moved to the more active and open NetBSD and FreeBSD teams. Others had jumped the fence to Linux. A lawsuit between Unix copyright holder AT&T and University of California at Berkeley was also partly to blame. But the Jolitzes, too, were criticized for their autocratic style. The strength of their convictions did not endear them to people who wanted to do things differently.“文章同时记载 386BSD 阵营对 FreeBSD/NetBSD/OpenBSD 的反控：”The xBSDs don't have any understanding of where we were heading,“says Lynne. ”They're looking to the past.”
- **与本章主题的关联说明**：本章 4.4BSD-Lite 发布事件须包含 386BSD 阵营对 USL 诉讼影响的陈述，Salon 专访是少数同时引用 386BSD 阵营（Jolitz 夫妇）与 FreeBSD/NetBSD 阵营的二手综合文献，构成本章 386BSD 阵营立场锚点。文章中“A lawsuit... was also partly to blame”陈述显示 386BSD 阵营将社区分化部分归因于 USL 诉讼。
- **与官方史料的交叉验证结论**：Salon 文章关于“USL 诉讼对 386BSD 用户社区分化的影响”的陈述与 McKusick 亲述“all the BSD groups that were doing releases at that time, BSDI, NetBSD, and FreeBSD, had to restart their code base with the 4.4BSD-Lite sources”一致；关于“386BSD 1.0 1993-12 发布”的陈述与 386BSD Release 0.1 1992-07-14 发布、386BSD Release 1.0 1993-12 发布的时序一致；关于“Jolitz 夫妇 autocratic style 的批评”为 386BSD 阵营单方陈述的反控，须与 FreeBSD/NetBSD 阵营对 Jolitz 夫妇的陈述交叉验证 ［考据注：2026-07-28 联网考据留痕，模板句式已按考据审慎原则精简（WO-004）；语义以正文与所引来源为准］。
