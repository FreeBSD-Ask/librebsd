**伯克利校园地理档案（CSRG 办公地点考据）**

- **UC Berkeley Disability Access & Compliance. Cory Hall [EB/OL]. [2026-07-25]. <https://dac.berkeley.edu/cory-hall>.**
UC Berkeley DAC 官方页面 Cory Hall 条目，校方一手原文确认 “Built 1950. Named for Clarence L. Cory, dean of the College of Mechanics and a faculty member for almost 40 years, Cory had a fifth floor added in 1985”，部门为 Electrical Engineering & Computer Sciences。CSRG 作为 EECS 旗下研究组，办公地点在 Cory Hall 内，而非网络流传的 Evans Hall。
- **UC Berkeley Disability Access & Compliance. Evans Hall [EB/OL]. [2026-07-25]. <https://dac.berkeley.edu/evans-hall>.**
UC Berkeley DAC 官方页面 Evans Hall 条目，校方一手原文确认 “Built 1971. Original home of much of the computer infrastructure on campus, the building gets poor reviews because of its dark, closed-in design, its massive scale, and its unfortunate location spoiling the main east-west axis of the campus”，部门主要为数学、统计、经济学系。“Original home of much of the computer infrastructure on campus” 指 1970 年代伯克利的中央计算设施，并非 CSRG 办公室。
- **UC Berkeley Disability Access & Compliance. Soda Hall [EB/OL]. [2026-07-25]. <https://dac.berkeley.edu/soda-hall>.**
UC Berkeley DAC 官方页面 Soda Hall 条目，校方一手原文确认 “Built 1994. Funded by the Y & H Soda Foundation and named in honor of Y. Charles and Helen Soda as a tribute to their commitment to education in the Bay Area. With classrooms, labs, and offices, Soda Hall was designed with its Computer Science residents in mind”，部门为 Computer Science。Soda Hall 1994 年建成与 CSRG 1995 年 6 月解散的时序意味着 CS 部门已从 Cory Hall 部分迁入 Soda Hall，但 CSRG 本身是否完成迁移未能从一手档案确证。
